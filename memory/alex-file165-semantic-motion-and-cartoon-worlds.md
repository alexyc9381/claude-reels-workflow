## Two layers of the many-models beat

Identity and quantity need different staging. The large leaders answer “which models?” The grouped smaller marks answer “how many options?” One shared receiving browser answers “where?” Keep those jobs distinct and timed to the narration. Fourteen authentic brand assets were reused for model options, not presented as fifty distinct companies. The verified catalogue had exactly 60 options at the recorded source snapshot; counts are not evergreen.

The outgoing browser at 10.467 seconds matched the incoming scene's position, shape and model positions. The spoken “place” and its caption continued through 10.55 seconds. Respect the spoken tail even when a visual cut occurs inside it.

These are delivered experiments, not proven winners or mandatory future themes. “Vending first” was an assistant recommendation, not Alex's selection. Genuine variants change causal action and composition, not just color or small timing differences.

## Posting caption and article are separate deliverables

Alex's singular “caption” correction referred to the written posting copy in this sequence. The first delivered copy began with a short CTA but omitted the rest of the saved structure. Following only the first-line rule is insufficient: quoted keyword CTA + actual named guide + follow reminder; READ BELOW as next non-empty line; useful numbered points; relevant context; repeated matching CTA; hashtags last. No em/en dashes. Check the actual uploaded file and the copy inside the source ZIP. See [canonical caption system](caption-structure.md).

The FILE article was already live at chen.media. We verified its title, successful HTTP response and canonical URL; no deployment or content update was performed. Do not describe this as a new publication. A working URL alone is not proof that every old claim is accurate. Existing concerns about response-speed and absolute-privacy wording remained documented; no article rewrite was part of this task.

## Review and delivery discipline

1. Repeated feedback at the same timestamp means revisit the concept, hierarchy or semantic action, not just increase motion metrics.
2. Use fresh storyboard critique before authoring and rendered visual critique afterward. Keep rejected concepts and the reason for changing them.
3. Inspect the exact criticized timestamps plus surrounding motion, native full frames, shot transitions and final carry. A strip is sampled evidence, not continuous playback.
4. Motion, luminance, cue-window energy and caption checks are diagnostics. They do not establish interestingness, pleasant sound, comprehension or retention. State actual listening/playback scope honestly.
5. Preserve the recorded voice, original sprite, approved caption track and body when only a hook is requested. Verify continuity against the previous source; do not rely on intent alone.
6. Update canonical Drive files in place for revisions, preserving IDs. Added hook alternatives get distinct files. Deliver playable full MP4s, not just source ZIPs or silent internal hook previews.
7. Verify cloud file size/hash metadata and visible Drive listing after sync. Local copy success alone is insufficient. Keep heavy media in Drive; commit text learnings and lightweight evidence to GitHub.
