# X144: repair the value gap around 22 seconds

Source: Alex's September 11, 2026 request in the X/SETUP production chat. He reported a
significant retention dip around 22 seconds in the PDF/MarkItDown reel, called it “X from
September 5,” and requested a revised video plus indexed GitHub learnings. The production
asset is the X144 PDF narration uploaded as `X sep 9.m4a`; subject and existing delivery
identify the intended reel despite the date-label difference.

## Evidence and uncertainty

**Reported:** retention dips hard at approximately 22 seconds. No retention chart, sample
size or controlled comparison was supplied. Do not manufacture percentages or causality.

**Observed in the previous delivered render:**

- 20.57–24.37s introduces MarkItDown using a repository card, FREE badge and star medallion.
  At 22s, the attention changes from the viewer's token problem to promotional proof.
- The following format scene first introduces a box. Its first PDF arrives about a second
  later. The dedicated conversion sequence does not start until 28.27s, roughly 7.7 seconds
  after the solution is introduced.
- The visual grammar repeatedly restarts: prop arrives, label resolves, prop holds.
  Claude's causal role diminishes during the credibility/display stretch.

**Working hypothesis:** naming the free solution resolves the initial question while the
animation delays the next practical payoff. Stars and repository display can feel like an
outro or advertisement. Repeated setup imposes another wait instead of making the answer
more useful. This fits the reported loss point but is not proven by it.

## Revision rules

1. Review at least 1–2 seconds **before** a reported cliff. Repair its lead-in, not only the
   frame at the timestamp. Here the rewrite starts at 20.57s, ahead of the reported 22s dip.
2. Introduce the tool **while it performs the promised operation**. A named converter should
   produce a useful result during its introduction; the logo is an identity anchor.
3. Keep GitHub stars a brief secondary detail, with meaningful work continuing beneath it.
   This revision uses a roughly 0.57s proof badge, not a prestige celebration.
4. Carry a causal thread across adjacent narration lines. File goes into the operating
   converter, unnecessary formatting separates, structure emerges, Claude catches the output,
   further formats enter the same mechanism, and the finished Markdown becomes the next beat.
5. A format list should demonstrate breadth without restarting with a box or five arrival-only
   cards. Keep the currently named format readable and show its consequence.
6. Inspect the **whole revised ending**. A stronger reveal cannot compensate for a later
   empty chatbox, passive progress bar or long CTA hold. Upload, tool call, conversion and
   opened file should be one visibly advancing interaction. Show the CTA keyword on entry.
7. Preserve the narration and approved voice treatment when the request concerns animation.
   Re-time contact SFX to the new operations; check voice intelligibility and actual stems.
8. Check actor/prop contact, scene tails, logo clearance, text at phone size and safe bounds.
   Gears alone do not constitute progress. A motion score may diagnose stillness but cannot
   establish recovered retention. No camera shake or ornamental churn to make it pass.

X remains authorized as a **visual-detail reference** for SETUP, not as an established
retention template. These are distinct judgments; see the
[SETUP scope clarification](alex-setup-2d-detail-and-performance.md#resolve-the-x-reference-correctly).
