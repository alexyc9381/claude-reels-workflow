# ⭐⭐⭐ A GRID FILLING IS ONE IDEA REPEATED N TIMES

Reel 125's hook took three rounds of making its OBJECTS move — they flew in, then
idled and chased lamps and fired jobs, then wired themselves to each other. Every
one was a real improvement. The note never changed.

> **Twenty things twitching in twenty fixed squares is a STATIC COMPOSITION with
> busy contents, and a viewer reads the composition first.**

The LAYOUT never moved. Every fix added motion *inside* a lattice that was itself
nailed down.

## ⭐ Make the layout the animation

Twenty marks on two **counter-rotating** rings, tumbling on their own axes,
spiralling in and locking into the grid — **four kinds of motion at once**
(ORBIT · TUMBLE · CONVERGE · ARRIVE). That is what *"more varying types of
movement"* means, and why none of it reads as a loop.

```
HOOK        11.72 → 18.94   weakest scene in the reel → strongest
0-1s FLOOR   4.77 → 25.04
```

⭐⭐ **THE FLOOR IS THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS.** Three rounds of adding motion moved
it 4.68 → 4.77; moving the layout moved it to 25. **When a note repeats after a
real improvement, the improvement was answering a different question.**

⛔ One ring of twenty turning together is a WHEEL, and a wheel is one idea —
counter-rotate.
⛔ Shrink the radius by ~0.55, never to zero: collapsing it fully pulls everything
through the centre and the middle of the move is an unreadable pile.
⛔ **Nobody checks the RATE.** That hook had been orbiting at 0.021 rad/frame =
**36°/sec**, a third of one revolution across a 3.5s shot, through seven rounds of
notes about what moves. Ramp it: `w0*f + a*f²`.
