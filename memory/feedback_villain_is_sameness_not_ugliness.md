⛔⛔⛔ Alex on reel 124, twice: *"i dont get why you keep havingt ht boring like gray thing that gets
stamped on his face like wtf / and the website on the stamper thing is not interesting its just
gray stuff."*

The VO said **every AI website builder gives you the same page**. I drew that page as a grey
wireframe. That was wrong twice over:

1. **GREY + RECTANGULAR is the named boring combination.** Motion is
   `(fraction repainted) × (luma delta)` — a grey slab on a grey ground is close to the
   lowest-scoring thing that can be drawn. See [[feedback_dressing_the_words_is_not_redoing_it]]
   and [[reference_motion_arithmetic]].
2. **It was FALSE.** Lovable and Replit ship a perfectly attractive landing page. I had drawn the
   competitor *badly* instead of *accurately*.

**Why:** the claim was never "the output is ugly" — it was "the output is **identical**." A picture
that argues the wrong claim cannot be fixed by making it prettier or adding motion, and a viewer who
has used the tool knows the frame is lying.

**How to apply:** name the exact property the line disparages, then build the picture around that
property. For *sameness*, draw the thing **well** — reel 124 used a real landing page (indigo/violet
gradient hero, orange CTA, three cards with teal/amber/rose icons) — and then repeat it identically
out of a press onto a growing stack. Repetition dramatises sameness and is free motion.
