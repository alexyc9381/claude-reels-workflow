# ⛔ RAISE THE BAR: breathtaking + cinematic, a real leg-up over past reels

> Alex, reel 62: *"the animations, backgrounds, shots and camera angles genuinely have to be breathtaking and
> cinematic — even more of a significant improvement over my previous videos."*

The clone-the-chassis rule ([[reel-clone-chassis-verbatim]]) still governs the **chrome** (caption engine, progress
bar, panel frame = house identity), BUT the **scene CONTENT** must be a cinematic step-up, not a reuse of the flat
sticker look. Treat the single dark panel as a **cinematic window / letterbox**, not a UI card.

**The four levers to hit every scene (bake in at storyboard time):**
1. **CAMERA** — a distinct MOVE per scene (dolly push-in, crane, orbit, snap pull-back, macro push, rack-focus). Cuts
   are camera transitions, not fades. The content transforms in 3D-feeling space.
2. **LIGHT** — volumetric + directional: warm key vs cool rim (the premium split), god-rays, inner glow, bloom on hot
   points, atmospheric haze/DOF. Never flat single-tone fills.
3. **DEPTH** — 4-6 parallax planes per scene (foreground silhouette → hero → mid → far nebula/starfield), each drifting
   at its own rate; DOF blur on fore/far; particles/embers.
4. **SCALE + BACKGROUNDS** — lean into epic scale contrast; backgrounds are RICH (layered cosmos gradient + nebula +
   starfield + drift), never a flat fill.
