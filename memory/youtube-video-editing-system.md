## Standing rules

- Remotion, never Supereditor. Separate 16:9 YouTube from the short-form reel chassis and retired Matchtern VSL style.
- OBS sole recorded narration; Sony muted. Preserve clap-measured offsets, complete words, useful teaching and context. Do not silently shorten a film by discarding useful content.
- One cumulative frame EDL for picture/audio/graphics/chapters. New source coverage requires corresponding camera-plate coverage.
- Screen large over cream/orange gradient; measured menu/Dock crop; typed field fully visible. Right rounded inset with independent crop; opening comparison may use bottom-center facecam.
- Per-frame person matte blurs room only, never face or a fixed oval. Fast-to-slow opening face push; smooth full-to-inset handoff.
- Mature Manrope sans-serif, white optical glass, orange-led color, strong contrast and centered safe hierarchy. No black glass, serif titles, competing abstract slab/ribbon, toy factories or empty generic rectangle/checkmark explainers.
- Distinct beginning → action → payoff for each explanation. Large original 2D sprites do real jobs; concurrent related supporting motion, natural contact/recovery, equal eye apertures. Existing role outfits are permitted by the later request.
- Do not repeat whole scenes. Shared rigs/materials are fine. Prefer actual UI/logos; never substitute a different video inside a result-review card.
- Meaningful technical definitions only, legible copy and orange perimeter timer; vary tips, brands, text lenses, chapters and full scenes. Never cover active typing.
- Original glowing CGI is preserved with provenance, but **removed from the current Higgsfield film** per later explicit feedback.
- Tactile quiet action SFX and finite music beneath real narration; no constant dings. Source-boundary repair before masking a problem with sound. Loudness tests do not prove listening quality.
- Keep A/B proof and cost/claim verification separate from polish. New narration needs Alex's recording; do not synthesize his voice or advertise an unspecified bonus.
- Deliver the full rendered video, not only the hook. Inspect current-source output, audio joins, end word, chapters and screen clearance.
- Code/docs/manifests in Git; raw private media and exports outside Git. Push does not mean publish video.
