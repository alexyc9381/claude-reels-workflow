Explicit user correction: captions around 23 seconds were too small with four adjacent words. Prevent recurrence by keeping a fixed large readable caption font, measuring the actual font, and splitting phrases rather than shrinking. A dangling connector or sentence-final orphan must never trigger a four-word merge. Preserve recorded word endings when switching groups. Validate actual on-screen caption grouping, not only the source transcript. This revision uses 84px Fraunces and a 890px advance budget; those are implementation values, not an instruction to redesign other videos.

JEV also requires action-driven visuals instead of small explanatory labels: stamp the prose, mark the paper, reveal new content under the moving lens, animate a concrete decision then confidence, fling uncertain cases to a human, and visualize the time/cost reduction. Remove decorative footers. Withhold COMMENT JEV across header, props and anticipatory captions until the CTA is spoken. Keep source/claim scope in post/article material when deleting the small footers.
