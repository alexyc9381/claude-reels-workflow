⛔⛔ Alex on reel 124: *"the face of the claude sprite like it should be more interestign compared to
whatever boring shape lands on its face right now."*

The audience reads the beat off the **face**. Landing a prop on it covers the one surface carrying
the emotion, at precisely the frame where the payoff happens — so the scene's event and the scene's
acting cancel each other out.

**Why:** the sprite is the actor, not a mounting point. A reel's emotional through-line is carried
by expression across scenes; occluding it on the beat frames removes the only continuous performance
in the piece and leaves moving shapes.

**How to apply:** land props **beside**, **in front of**, or **into the hands of** the sprite and let
the face **react**. Nothing — prop, plate, chip or header — occludes the face on a beat frame. Give
the face its own authored change on the beat (widen, squint, brow) rather than relying on the prop
to carry it.

**And headers name the OUTCOME in the viewer's words**, not the set and not the theme. Alex rewrote
mine himself: *"the header text needs to be a lot better like \"Create 3D AI Websites\""* — the thing
the viewer wants to be able to *do*. See [[feedback_headers_state_the_claim]].
