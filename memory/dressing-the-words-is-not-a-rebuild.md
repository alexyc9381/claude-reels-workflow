# ⛔⛔⛔ A new substrate is not a new scene

Reel 120's S1 carries *"And Anthropic, the creators of Claude, even admitted it."* It was
rejected **twice**:

- **v1** — a three-face prism rolls over to a cream card with the test name on it.
  *"at 5 seconds its too much of a text animation so its not good enough like too boring -
  needs to be redone completely."*
- **v2** — a CAST METAL PLATE swings in 672px on a chain and is RIVETED on: four hits, sparks,
  a lamp, a real seat. *"at 5 seconds that animation with just the tons of text and stuff is way
  too boring like i dont like that completely redo that animation scene."*

v2 had travel, four costed arrivals and real drawing, and it was **the same scene**, because the
words were still the thing you were looking at. Riveting a paragraph to a wall is a paragraph on
a wall.

⭐ **THE TEST, BEFORE BUILDING: if you muted the copy, what would be happening?** v1 and v2 both
answered *"a rectangle arrives."*

## ⭐⭐ Dramatise the FACT, then shrink the receipt to label size

Anthropic's system cards evaluate models on reward-hack-prone coding tasks — i.e. *the makers
run a test for this*. So v3 is a **bench test**: a Claude clamped in a cradle, a scan head
sweeping down him, a big needle swinging out of the green and slamming into the red, a beacon,
the room going red, and the two makers watching. The test name survives as a **17px stencil on
the rig**, the size a model number actually is. Same first-party receipt, no longer the subject.

⛔ **AND THE SWEEP HAD TO SWEEP.** v3's first cut ran the scan head down in 8 frames and parked
it: **74% hold** on a 69-frame scene. Driving it off `0.5 - 0.5*cos(t)` instead of a one-shot
ease gave one and a half passes of a 300×62 bar — hold **74% → 43%**.
See [[authored-motion-needs-its-own-driver]].

## ⭐ The same note, one scene later, about a PROP

*"the thing that comes out of the boxes needs to be more interesting and spark more curiosity
and like that thing is just way too gray and boring."* That was the shared grey slab for the
third time. Replaced with a brass machine with a **domed** silhouette, six turning valve wheels,
live gauges and a **backlit glowing nameplate**.

⭐ **GREY + RECTANGULAR is the combination that reads as boring. Either one alone survives.**
