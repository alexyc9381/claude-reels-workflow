## Revision sequence and issues caught

1. Rebuilt the requested body concepts and added portrait rings; rendered a half-size full preview/contact sheet.
2. Corrected cup-dive actor clipping and saucer geometry, separated race ratio/descriptor from driver, removed receipt's rectangular masking patch, kept target continuous into arrow contact, expanded sparse pizza payoff to eight staggered boxes.
3. Rendered both hook previews and inspected their different mechanisms. Full renders followed.
4. Refined robot grip and coordinate transforms/stepping, completed cup's return to the real tabletop, and made the target flip into a medal. Rendered the final main body and reviewed contact frames at 18.38, 20.35, 21.03, 21.4, 25.3, 28.8, 30.27 and 30.65s.
5. Encoded all variants with final main body from frame 153 onward and each matching master WAV, avoiding stale trial bodies. Rebuilt README, storyboard, review notes and editable ZIP after final changes. Archived old review locally and omitted obsolete audio stems from delivery ZIP.

The earlier initial delivery had six self-review passes and five motion-flagged body scenes. Alex still rejected many concepts. The new delivery improved the concepts and reduced main's flagged count to two, but does not establish that the quality target was met.
