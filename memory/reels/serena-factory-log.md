### ⭐⭐ HOOK v8 — "the whole screen is just shaking" (2026-07-25, after delivery)
Alex on the shipped cut: *"the opening scene is very odd, it's just a bunch of shaking, the whole screen is shaking,
which is very uncomfortable to continue watching, and there's not much going on."* THREE real defects found by reading
the code, not by taste:
1. ⛔⛔ **FULL-FRAME SHAKE.** `tremble = sin(lf*3.1)*2.2 + sin(lf*7.3)*1.1` and `s2shake = sin(f2*2.6)*4.6 +
   sin(f2*6.2)*1.8`, each applied to a container with `inset:0` — i.e. **the entire scene**, at ~12-15Hz with a ~30Hz
   component, up to **±6.4px**. That is a buzz, not weight. An agent asked for "strain" strapped it to the CAMERA
   instead of the object. ⭐ **RULE: never translate an `inset:0` container. Strain wobble goes on the OBJECT only,
   period >= 1s, amplitude <= 2px.**
2. ⭐⭐ **THE MOTION METRIC WAS FOOLED BY IT.** The frame-diff audit scored S1 the HIGHEST in the reel
   (17.4/12.4/9.9/23.6) while it was the least watchable, because pixel-differencing cannot tell narrative movement
   from a vibrating frame. My "zero dead seconds" pass therefore certified the worst scene. ⭐ **The audit is a FLOOR
   test only — it can prove a scene is dead, never that it is good. Always eyeball the hook.** After the fix S1 reads
   14.3/9.0/13.6/8.5/5.9... = real motion.
3. ⛔ **ARMS.** The custom Atlas arms kept rendering after the globe shrank, so they stuck up off his head like orange
   horns, and they sat over the brazier flames. Fix: gate the arms on `bear > 0` and push the braziers to cx±322.
