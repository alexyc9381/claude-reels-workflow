### ⛔ THE SOURCING TRUTH — the 36x is a ChatGPT-TAM mirage, and I will not launder it
The headline (36.29x / 9.49M / 1.98% comment-gated) is **real** but it is a **ChatGPT** reel. When the SAME creator ran
the SAME format **on Claude, keyword-gated**, it did **0.64x and 0.70x — below his own median**, twice. His Claude
skills listicle (the comp we shipped as SKILLS-51) did **0.82x**. The only Claude "codes" reel that beat median
(7.06x) was **ungated spectacle**, not our lead-magnet mechanic. **Read:** the 36x is audience *size* (ChatGPT ≈ 10x
Claude's TAM), not the mechanic. The mechanic on Claude, gated, has a **track record of under-median.** That is the
real baseline reel 62 is betting against — not 36x.

### transfer_hypothesis (REQUIRED FIELD — stated as a hypothesis, not a finding)
"Secret codes/commands" pulls because it promises a **free, instant, paste-able capability jump** with zero setup —
the lowest-friction value promise in AI content, and comment-gated because the audience wants *the rest of the list*.
That mechanism transfers to a Claude-native account IF (and only if) the codes shown are **real Claude features a
normal user didn't know exist** — because our audience is *already* Claude users, so mavgpt's TAM penalty doesn't
apply to us, but our audience will instantly smell a fake/prompt-101 "code." The bet is: on-platform credibility
(real commands, captured working) converts the format's proven curiosity pull into a save. **Transfer risk: LOW on
format, HIGH on content authenticity (see the innovation-bar wound below).**
