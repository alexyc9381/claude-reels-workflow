## The subject, and where the frame stops

Anthropic, **2026-08-25**: memory is now a set of individual **files** grouped
under **Topics** in Memory settings, written **as you chat** instead of summarised
after a conversation ends; you can read, edit or delete each one; it is on by
default on **Free, Pro and Max** across web, desktop and mobile; and chat and
**Cowork** share one memory, with Cowork tasks running in the cloud.

⛔ **The VO says "for all users" and the picture does not.** Team/Enterprise
memory is admin-controlled and defaults OFF for individuals, so every plate
carries `R.plans` = `FREE · PRO · MAX`. Standing rule: when a VO asserts a result
you cannot source, dramatise the MECHANISM and stop at the edge of the claim.

⛔ **"Delete Obsidian" is the VO's hyperbole.** The tower carries the real
Obsidian mark because it is the noun in the spoken line, but nothing on screen
says the product is worse or broken — it is a vault you fill BY HAND, and what
replaces it is one that fills itself. That is the whole comparison.

## The world

**THE MEMORY WORKS** — a records **plant**, not a library. Nothing is browsed;
everything is MADE, continuously, while the hero is mid-sentence. That is the
subject: the news is not that Claude has storage, it is that the writing happens
DURING the conversation. A library would have said the opposite thing, and it
would have repeated reel 112 THE STACKS.

**Villain: THE GAP**, the black slot between one chat and the next. Eats the
details at S3, wins again at S4, is **plated over** at S5 (floored, not killed),
and **re-opens under the severed local line at S14 and wins there.** The reel's
opening problem is its closing caveat, in the same physical object.
