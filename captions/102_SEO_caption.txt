The mechanism is the interesting part. Most SEO tools hand you a list of 40 problems, which is the same as handing you nothing, because you do not know which one is upstream of the others. This one sequences them into a dependency graph and emits a prioritised plan. Fix number one, and several of the others resolve on their own.

The part nobody mentions: it is an analysis plugin, not an autopilot. It GENERATES your fixes, the JSON-LD, the sitemaps, the hreflang blocks, the content briefs, and Claude Code can apply them to your source. It does not log into your CMS and rewrite your live site, and anyone telling you otherwise has not run it. Same for "trained on Google's guidelines". Nothing is trained. The recommendations are grounded in Google's own published docs, including the AI Optimization Guide, and every single one ships with an explicit check for how you would know it failed. That is a much better property than being trained on anything.
