The mechanism is not that it makes the prompt longer. It makes every word load bearing. Most wasted messages are not caused by the model being wrong, they are caused by the model being right about a request that was underspecified: no success criteria, no scope boundary, no stack, no stop condition. So it does exactly what it was told, you look at it, and you spend three more messages explaining what you actually meant. Four attempts for one result, every time.

The part nobody mentions: the expensive failure is not the bad answer, it is the re-prompt. You already paid for attempt one. Then you paid again to describe attempt one. Then again. Nobody counts those, because each one feels like a small correction rather than a bill, and that is exactly why the total is invisible.
