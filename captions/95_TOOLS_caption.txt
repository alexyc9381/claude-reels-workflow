CC0 is the part most people miss. That is not MIT. It is a public domain dedication: no licence terms, no attribution, nothing to comply with. You can paste any of it anywhere.

There is nothing to install. No app, no CLI, no skill. It is 184 markdown files and you clone the folder.

The part nobody mentions: a system prompt is written for a specific model with specific tools. Pasting Claude's instructions into a small open model gives you Claude's INSTRUCTIONS, not Claude's capabilities. Strip every line that references a tool your model does not have, keep the skeleton, and test it against your own task before you believe anything.

And even if you never swap a model, read three of these back to back. They are the most carefully edited prompts in the industry, written by teams with millions of users and real liability. Watch how few refusal rules there are, how early the output format is pinned, and how much of each one is spent on what NOT to do.
