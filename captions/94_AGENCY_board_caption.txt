Read those counts before you install anything. They tell you where the repo is deep and where it is thin. Engineering and Specialized are more than half the roster on their own. Healthcare is three files. If you are shipping software, two divisions cover you. If you are running marketing, Marketing plus Paid Media is 43 agents and you will not get through them in a month.

Ad Creative Strategist sits in Paid Media. Reddit Community Builder sits in Marketing, and its brief tells it that it is not marketing on Reddit, it is becoming a valued community member who happens to represent a brand.

The desktop app is a second free repo. Native for macOS, Windows and Linux, it browses the roster and installs into Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Qwen and Osaurus, then keeps them updated. No clone, no terminal.

The part nobody mentions: the installer takes a division filter, and almost nobody uses it. Run the list command first, look at the counts above, install two divisions, and give them a week before you add a third. An agent you have never read is not a team member, it is a file.
