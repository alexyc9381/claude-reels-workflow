The reason this matters is not the count. It is that the hard part of automation was never the idea, it was the ninety minutes of wiring nodes together and finding out on step eleven that the auth is wrong. Somebody else already ate that. You are picking a finished machine off a shelf.

Where to start, honestly. Do not import twenty of them. Pick the one job you do by hand every single day, find the folder it lives in, and run that one. Get it working end to end before you touch a second. The people who get nothing out of repos like this are the ones who import forty workflows on a Sunday and never wire the credentials for any of them.

Two honest notes.

One: these are community templates, not a product. Some are excellent, some are two years old, a few reference nodes that have since changed. Read the workflow before you activate it, especially anything that sends email or posts on your behalf.

Two: free means the templates, not the running. n8n itself is free to self-host and there is a paid cloud tier, and the model calls inside any AI workflow still cost whatever the model costs. Budget for that before you wire up something that runs every fifteen minutes.
