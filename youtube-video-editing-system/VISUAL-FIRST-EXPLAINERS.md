# Visual-first explanations and intro promise

Latest direction, September 12, 2026. Applies to future No Code Alex YouTube scene design; it does not certify that every historical scene has been rebuilt.

## Graphic storytelling

Use recognizable objects and meaningful character actions, not paragraphs inside decorative rectangles. Reuse the original No Code Alex 2D Claude rig, equal eyes, role outfits, warm brand palette and Manrope. An editorial Higgsfield counterpart may operate relevant props while retaining the real logo; it is not an official mascot.

Each scene needs a beginning, a consequential action and a different final state. A calendar turning communicates recurrence; a request traveling through a skill communicates per-use generation; a guidebook instruction physically copied into a prompt field communicates follow-along. Move supporting objects with the primary action, using anticipation, arcs, contact recovery and damped settling. Faster pacing does not mean every object oscillates independently.

Mute the scene and hide nonessential text during review. Can a viewer still identify the subject and change? Keep only useful labels, prices with units, commands, numbered navigation and necessary qualifications. Do not remove factual caveats just to achieve a text quota. Full-scene headings and slogan subheaders are not default requirements.

Keep one dominant action, room for the presenter, readable real footage, and clear prop silhouettes at entrance/contact/exit extremes. White glass is a material treatment, not permission to put every thought in another card. A stage or background should support depth without adding unrelated machinery.

## First-five-second title/thumbnail continuity

Obtain the exact title and thumbnail before claiming a verified packaging match. Neither was supplied for V15. The working assumption is a less-expensive Claude-skill alternative to subscription video generation, with a blind quality comparison.

Recommended treatment: make the first frame the thumbnail in motion. Match its clip placement, color and visual identities, then start both large clips immediately. Keep circular 1/2 identities and the lower-center presenter. Do not attach prices or company identities to the blind clips before the reveal.

If the title leads with cost, one brief neutral comparison cue can name the two approaches above the blind clips without assigning identities. If the title leads with quality, synchronized visual action and the question are the first proof. If it leads with a free skill, preview the actual skill invocation and result soon afterward; do not imply free API generation. These are options pending the actual packaging, not three overlays to stack together.

At 0, 1, 3 and 5 seconds, inspect without audio: can a new viewer identify the comparison, see the outputs clearly, and connect it to the clicked promise? Then check the first 30 seconds for actual evidence of delivery. Retention is an editorial hypothesis until audience data exists.

## Proof boundaries

Actual OBS excerpts establish that the recorded workflow exists. A condensed command → prompt → result montage is not a real-time generation benchmark, a matched-price receipt, a guarantee of equal quality, or proof of replacing every platform feature. Label condensed footage. Never manufacture terminal text or stitch unrelated claims into narration.

A monthly plan and a per-generation cost are different units. Retain credits/model variability and do not derive a savings multiplier without equivalent generation settings and source receipts. Confirm the final download link and exact supported installation paths before publishing.
