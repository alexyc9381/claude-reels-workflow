# Content-first long-form editing

Do not optimize a tutorial to a short runtime at the expense of its explanation. A fast hook and deliberate motion do not require removing the teaching steps. This applies to the No Code Alex YouTube Remotion workflow, including Higgsfield Replacement.

## Before shortening the source

1. Read the full-source transcript, not just the current selects. Divide it into unique ideas, demonstrations, bridges, retakes, and waits.
2. Give each unique idea a disposition: KEEP, CONDENSE, DUPLICATE TAKE, PICKUP NEEDED, CLAIM REVIEW, or DISCARD WITH REASON. Preserve source timecodes. A claim needing evidence is not automatically filler.
3. Map the viewer's path: why this matters → how it works → prerequisites → setup → action → result → next step. Do not remove a prerequisite, explanation, caveat, or promised payoff without recording how that job is still fulfilled.
4. Preserve the best complete take of each useful idea. A later setup restart supersedes an earlier duplicate, not every useful explanation before it.
5. Check source coverage against the assembled transcript before the graphics pass. Report significant omissions and genuinely missing pickups to Alex.

## Speech boundaries

- VAD identifies review candidates; it does not decide final word or sentence boundaries.
- Do not apply a universal maximum silence duration to demonstrations or generated-result playback. Review the picture as well as the audio.
- Use complete phrases with reviewed onset/release handles. Start around 150–250 ms before speech and 200–350 ms after the last audible phoneme where the source permits, then adjust by listening. These are review starting points, not automatic trims. Do not extend a handle into a retake marker or the next aborted sentence.
- Transcriber word times may stretch words across silence or omit “cut cut.” Check acoustic evidence and audition joins; a clean ASR transcript is not proof of a clean splice.
- Reject isolated fragments created by automatic segmentation. Never keep a half-second “going to…” clip between otherwise complete statements.
- J-cuts change picture/sound timing, not the spoken meaning. Never use a transition or music to conceal a missing word or unfinished thought.

## Proof and pickups

- Distinguish missing source footage from material removed by the editor. Recover existing good material first.
- Keep unverified price, quality, ownership, and comparison claims in a review ledger. Resolve them before publication; do not silently rewrite Alex's recorded meaning.
- Pickups should supply a missing mechanism, prerequisite, qualification, bridge, or fair comparison. Provide the exact proposed line, insertion point, visual treatment, and any evidence/input needed.
- OBS remains the sole recorded narration master. Sony clips remain muted. No synthesized replacement narration without explicit authorization.
- Preserve earlier exports and manifests. Do not call a recovery plan an updated video, or a render/decode check a full listening review.

## Finish gate

- Every useful unique source idea is accounted for.
- All kept sentences have complete starts and ends, and explicit retake markers are absent.
- The tutorial can be followed without guessing an omitted setup step.
- Chapter and graphics cues are recalculated from the repaired timeline.
- Running time follows the complete story unless Alex sets a genuine duration constraint.
