## Practical review sequence

1. Resolve source copies, complete lesson coverage, sync and paired cuts.
2. Establish visual/motion language on a short opening review when needed, then expand coherent sections and deliver the requested full scope.
3. Make narration-to-action map with entity, action, result and sound event per scene. Inspect later scenes for static holds and repeats.
4. Review mixed audio, specific joins, source logos, module readability and actual cursor-target coordinates in rendered frames/sequences.
5. Verify final speech endpoint, CTA wording, frame count, decode, timestamps and continuous mix. Save evidence, preserve prior versions, deliver the actual file.
