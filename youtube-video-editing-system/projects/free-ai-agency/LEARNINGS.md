# Free AI Agency in Claude: conversation learnings

Recorded September 16, 2026. These are the user's requested direction and observed editing failures, not a claim of approval of every exported scene. Latest corrections supersede earlier experiments. Follow the current Remotion system in this repository.

## Speech and editorial integrity

- Preserve all useful spoken teaching and script deviations. The script is a guide, not an exact transcript or permission to shorten the lesson. A dramatically shortened 6:13 cut was rejected for removing good material.
- Remove failed "cut cut" takes and genuine dead air. Never include the failed take instead of the successful one. Review the ending explicitly.
- Close internal pauses, including the repeatedly reported pauses around 7–8 seconds, 21 seconds, 2:44, 2:51 and 4:29. Recheck timing after cuts because user timestamps refer to the version they reviewed.
- Protect complete words and consonant tails: advice, visuals, competitors and visibility were specifically clipped. Listen across both sides of edits, including the complete sentence around 4:35.
- Do not join the first half of one take to the second half of another when a clean continuous take exists. Keep normal speech speed and original voice.
- Camera-only lines can be enhanced through Adobe Podcast when authorized. Match the enhanced audio's timing and loudness to the external microphone. Avoid echo, doubled tracks and synthetic replacement speech.
- Do not append a silent or animation-only outro beyond the last spoken word.

## Presenter and layout

- Keep the webcam visible beside the animations as the normal layout, including the GEO walkthrough around 4:50. The attempt to hide it for an entire minute was explicitly rejected.
- Cover verified glances or mismatched camera/audio takes with short intentional cutaways only. Review the entire cutaway, both boundaries and adjacent frames; prevent one-frame webcam flashes and repeated on/off layout changes.
- Cover the grey bed using the established framing/background treatment. A warm illustrative replacement was rejected; retain the recognizable structured system.
- Use stable layer isolation and z-order. Do not let animation content bleed behind or over the presenter. Keep sprite opacity solid; animate the work instead of flashing the character.
- Give text, sprites, badges and illustrations explicit safe areas. Prior overlaps were reported at 0:25, 1:02 and 1:26. Avoid low-contrast text and logos.

## Motion and communication

- Every scene needs a clear beginning, action and result tied to the actual voiceover. An entrance followed by a static hold is insufficient. The hook should develop rapidly without switching so fast that viewers cannot understand it.
- Prefer concrete visual work over large blocks of text, repeated rectangles, generic icons, abstract circles or repeated stock/generated photographs. Vary scene structure across the lesson.
- Claude sprites must perform each job: research leads, scan pages, create marketing assets, generate personalized email, validate data and deliver finished work. Give sales, SEO, marketing and AI visibility visibly distinct outfits and held tools.
- Show company logos when the companies are named, including Claude, ChatGPT/OpenAI, Google and Perplexity. Ensure contrast on dark backgrounds.
- Trace an orange/clay ring around the exact department or output as it is spoken: marketing, sales, SEO, website, emails, ads, 30 days, content and connected departments. Ripple the sales pipeline when it is named.
- Cursors must visibly move to their target, click with a click animation, and cause a UI response.
- Use recognizable Claude chat composition rather than an empty rectangle. Show email generation as processing followed by a personalized finished result.
- Show GEO content as scanning content and improving questions, direct answers and supporting evidence for AI. Explain GEO briefly as generative engine optimization when introduced.
- Show structured data as extracting business details with explicit meaning; show website crawlers following pages and checking access, not just decorative connector lines.
- Use realistic contact headshots. Generated med-spa imagery should be varied and useful, including social posts, but not repeated everywhere. Small-screen assets do not justify excessive image-generation spending.

## Tutorial footage and chapter design

- When saying "this GitHub repo," show the actual repository, highlight its name and zoom to it. Show the scroll to "one command install," then highlight that command. Use a visible cursor and click response.
- Keep section context clear and scrubbable. Keyword indicators should help identify the topic, not become a large text-heavy overlay. Remove unnecessary headers such as "Your AI agency" and cluttered keyword badges.
- Chapter transitions should follow the professional Claude Beginners system. Use large readable titles, purposeful sprite outfits, and alternating cream and muted clay-orange cards with appropriate contrast. Avoid harsh black-on-orange slabs.
- No em dashes in on-screen copy.
- Begin with a fast controlled digital dolly-style zoom; maintain comfortable face framing.

## Music and sound effects

- Musical intensity comes from the composition, not volume. Use tension in the hook or stakes, release when the answer lands, lighter instrumental tutorial sections, deliberate silence for insights, and a resolved close inside the spoken timeline.
- Change cues to mark sections; avoid vocals and busy melodies. The initial dull bed and subsequent overly loud beds were rejected.
- The latest mix lowers music alone by another 6 dB while keeping dialogue and SFX unchanged. Measured active-speech separation is approximately 30.6 dB in this export; this is a project measurement, not a universal loudness target.
- Use varied subtle, action-timed clicks, pops and whooshes. Remove annoying whistles and repetitive pencil/scan sounds. SFX were reduced by 5 dB during review; do not make every event louder to create energy.
- Keep music attribution with the project and supply it for the YouTube description.

## Delivery, storage and validation

- Deliver actual rendered revisions, not only source changes or an audio-only update when visual changes were requested.
- Re-render changed picture ranges only; reuse unchanged encoded sections. For audio-only revisions, copy the video bitstream and verify it remains identical.
- Retain one current final and essential edit/source assets. Remove reproducible previews and temporary renders after validation. No unwanted small camera proxy or continued Drive upload.
- Keep a complete render manifest before starting an incremental update. A stopped render must not discard the prior completed chunk boundaries; preserve a backup for recovery.
- Verify frame count, duration, audio synchronization, speech endings and changed boundaries. Inspect actual exported frames, not only Remotion source previews. Technical checks do not establish artistic approval.
- Embed the playable final in chat and reveal it selected in Finder for manual YouTube upload. Do not upload to YouTube merely because the file is ready.
- The user explicitly authorized pushing the learnings from this chat to main. Do not push raw footage, rendered movies, private machine paths or bulky temporary assets with the learning record.
