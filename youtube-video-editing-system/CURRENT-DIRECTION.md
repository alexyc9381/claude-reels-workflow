# Current No Code Alex YouTube direction

Consolidated September 11, 2026 from Alex's full system-building and Higgsfield revision conversation. This is the current editorial contract. A user-requested direction is not proof that a particular render has received creative approval. New explicit feedback takes precedence over this document; record that change in the decision ledger.

## Scope and engine

- **No Code Alex YouTube editing system. Remotion, not Supereditor.** Keep the long-form system separate from Matchtern and from the 9:16 short-form chassis.
- Reuse the No Code Alex 2D animation library, brand materials and meaningful character acting. Do not clone every short-form scene or enforce a seven-second duration on long-form explanations.
- Course-system transfers are enumerated in [TRANSFER-LOG.md](TRANSFER-LOG.md): voice-led editing, one EDL, phrase protection, chrome crop, privacy, silent-demo exceptions, measured sync, module boundaries and audio verification. Course-specific anti-graphics rules were not imported into YouTube.
- Code, structured edits, source checksums, learning notes and verification receipts belong in Git. Raw footage, private credentials, music files, generated assets, caches and exports do not.

## Narrative and retention

- Start from what Alex actually said. Preserve useful teaching, context, caveats, natural phrase tails and bridges. Do not manufacture a punchier claim by joining unrelated takes.
- Remove retakes, spoken editor markers, accidental dead air and explicitly requested repetition. A whole-file transcript saying no “cut” is not proof; inspect short original-source windows and final joins.
- Keep the opening comparison large and immediate; first five seconds especially active, first fifteen brisk. Action and curiosity take priority over labels and generic scene titles. Never reveal the answer before the question earns it.
- A later teaser uses **playing** result footage, moderate blur, readable black type on white glass and the original reaction. No “Wait until you see this” badge or teaser progress bar.
- A roadmap should be an actual traversable route with meaningful stops. Chapters are complete tasks, shown through PART cards and final-timeline scrub timestamps, not arbitrary durations.
- Explain each new concept with a different beginning → action → payoff. Remove a visual that does not support the sentence. Do not fill every moment simply to satisfy a motion count.
- A retention audit is editorial judgment, not measured audience retention. Keep unresolved proof/claim gaps and proposed pickups separate from animation polish. Do not erase useful content to hit a shorter runtime.
- Late tutorial completion is a possible exit point: tease the real next useful resource before the reveal/CTA. Do not promise an unspecified bonus. The current film can mention its free guide and existing skill file; a distinct bonus awaits its actual identity.

## Framing and readability

- Large screen recording over a branded cream/orange gradient; maximize readable UI inside landscape safe margins. Measure and remove the source menu/time bar and Dock; never crop the typing box off-screen.
- Substantial rounded facecam on the **right**, except the opening comparison's bottom-center presenter. Full-face and inset framing are independent. Full-frame crop hides the bed; inset retains torso and centers the face. Latest front-facing intro request crops more from the left.
- Opening face push accelerates quickly then gently settles. Full-face → demo uses shared geometry: slight twist/scale and travel down to the right inset, with playback continuous across the handoff.
- Blur the **room**, not the face. Use per-frame person segmentation or a verified mask; no fixed oval pretending to track the subject. Do not repeatedly intensify blur without checking its boundary and skin detail.
- Follow the actual spoken/cursor target with a reviewed smooth zoom. A text lens uses synchronized original OBS pixels, not retyped or invented text. Move the inset and helpers away while typing.
- Default safe stage at 1080p is roughly x=110…1810, y=80…1000; opening A/B clips may use the intentionally reviewed wider layout. Also reserve the moving facecam's swept area. Inspect entrance/exit extremes, not only a centered still.
- A supporting card must not show a different result than the main screen. Prefer annotating the real playing result. If duplicating footage, use exactly the same asset and source clock.

## Art direction and motion

- Mature, polished, cinematic, professional—not childlike toy factories. More interesting means stronger concepts, recognizable objects, purposeful mechanisms and detailed scenes, not more arbitrary rectangles, circles or checkmarks.
- **Manrope sans-serif** for authored long-form graphics. No serif fallback, Fraunces display headings, unexplained schematic labels, persistent “No Code Alex / How it works” headers or slogan subheaders.
- **White translucent optical glass only.** Warm orange rim, reflected highlights, readable pale fill, restrained refraction/blur, light/shadow separation. No black glass. Black/dark type on pale surfaces; examine the actual dark/light footage underneath.
- Orange/cream lead. Teal, blue, mint and gold support hierarchy. Preserve the original orange sprite identity. No competing abstract slab/ribbon; an oval stage is acceptable. Populated sets should support the workflow, not obstruct text.
- One dominant action, with concurrent related supporting jobs. Make moving subjects large enough to follow and keep the composition centered/grouped. Not a tiny isolated card, and not scattered equal-weight activity.
- Actors need jobs: carry, operate, inspect, hand over, receive, place, direct. Props respond to those actions. Keep object identity through a handoff and show a visible final state different from the beginning.
- Natural acceleration/deceleration, anticipation, arcs, contact absorption, recoil, damped follow-through and settling. No hard-stop landings. Environmental motion must not substitute for missing primary action.
- Keep original 2D Claude anatomy and **equal eye apertures**. Use gaze, blink, concentration and pleased recovery; no permanently enlarged single eye. Existing courier/archivist/operator outfits are allowed by the later explicit request.
- Shared primitives are allowed; **replaying whole animation scenes is not**. Vary layout, job, mechanism, action and payoff. A character change or a new title on the same scene is not a new concept.
- Key-term cards explain genuinely useful technical terms, not basic phrases such as “fine detail.” Large, well-filled text area; orange rounded-perimeter timer completes before the card fades. Keep the screen demo present beneath them.
- Pro tips, brand marks, chapter navigation, text lenses and full explainers have distinct functions and varied placements. Use real logos where available; an illustrative lens is not a replacement brand mark.

## Sound and sync

- **OBS is the only recorded narration source. Sony remains muted.** Clap/scratch audio can establish camera offsets, never replace final OBS audio. Preserve measured offsets and source-frame alignment across revisions.
- Protect whole words at both boundaries. Repair the source range first; don't hide a damaged phrase with SFX. A new voiceover requires Alex's recording; do not synthesize his voice or claim he spoke an overlay's new sentence.
- Use picture-led J-cuts where they help continuity without desynchronizing the speaking face. Keep OBS, camera, chapter and graphics timing derived from one cumulative frame EDL.
- Sound follows action: paper, contacts, control clicks, typing, restrained motion and latch sounds. No automatic ding for every card, no cue on every blink/gesture, no loud effects over the VO.
- Apply [SOUND-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md](SOUND-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md): purpose, envelope, pitch, purposeful layers, simplicity, cleaning, tone, size, movement and depth. Mix under the **actual narration**, not an isolated audition.
- Finite musical sections can support hook/tension/payoff. They must not compete with speech. Source licensing and required credits remain publication gates; a premium sound is not established by loudness metrics.
- Render one continuous narration/design mix, not separately encoded AAC per picture chunk. Check decoded final audio, target phrase joins, true peak, chapters and sampled source alignment. End on the completed last word without a frozen silent tail.

## Delivery and authority

- Apply full-video requests across the entire timeline. An intro-only preview is not the deliverable.
- Review opening, dense middle, real demo, changed cuts and closing. Use tests plus rendered frames/action sequences; tests cannot certify artistic quality or listening approval.
- Current review target is 1920×1080/30 from 1080p OBS. Use a 4K master only where source quality supports it; upscaling is not new detail.
- Link/embed the actual completed full video in chat. State what remains unrecorded, unverified or unapproved. Preserve previous exports and source recordings.
- A code/documentation GitHub push is distinct from publishing the video. Never silently upload raw private footage or publish a review as final.
