# Talking-head YouTube profile

Use when Alex's presentation is the narrative spine and screens, B-roll, or graphics provide evidence and resets.

## Assembly

- Preserve natural delivery. Remove failed takes, duplicates, explicit markers, long dead air, and obvious setup material.
- The later complete take usually supersedes an earlier false start, but the final selection is verified against meaning and delivery.
- Cut boundaries land in measured quiet or at an earned picture change; do not clip consonants to make the pace feel faster.

## Visual system

- Keep the presenter visible when expression or trust carries the beat.
- Use real UI, screenshots, footage, and data when the narration makes a factual claim.
- Full-screen cutaways must clarify, prove, demonstrate, compare, or reset attention. Decoration alone does not earn a cut.
- Avoid several consecutive beats with the same layout or representation mode.
- Captions are optional and landscape-specific; do not import the 9:16 karaoke system.

## Sound

- Dialogue owns the mix.
- Music and effects support section energy and visible actions without flattening the presenter.
- Inspect stems at problem timestamps; a full-mix loudness number cannot identify what masked a word.

## Gates

- The opening establishes a concrete promise and earns the next section.
- Every cutaway has a stated editorial purpose.
- Claims shown on screen match the spoken recording and current source.
- The final marker, privacy, caption, audio, and playback checks pass.
