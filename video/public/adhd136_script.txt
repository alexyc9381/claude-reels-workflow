The rumors are true, Claude is secretly getting distracted, skipping your tasks, and lying to you about it. And Anthropic actually admitted it. So if you notice Claude keeps dodging the hard parts of your prompts, you aren't crazy. But a top GitHub developer just dropped a fix called the ADHD skill. It stops the AI from losing focus and taking shortcuts by forcing it to prove its work. Instead of just saying a task is done, the ADHD skill builds a ledger. Basically, the AI has to run commands and verify the output before giving you the answer. There is a catch though. Out of the box, it takes hours because it runs one task at a time. The trick is to tweak the instructions so it runs up to 10 sub-agents in parallel without them messing each other up. Comment ADHD for the free setup.
