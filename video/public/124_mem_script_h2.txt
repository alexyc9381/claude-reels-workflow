Most people don't realize that Anthropic just released an official second brain for Claude.
This is insane because it now automatically remembers your projects and preferences across every single conversation.
If you use Claude you know how annoying it is when it keeps forgetting important details every time you switch chats.
Now Claude saves details dynamically in the background.
So you can go into your settings and literally see your memories categorized by topic.
You can click on any file to edit the details or delete anything you want it to forget.
It even works in your browser across the desktop app and even on mobile for all users.
Now this completely changes how you use Claude but there's one major catch you need to know before you turn this on.
If you set your tasks to run locally on your machine the memory won't sync because it only works in the cloud.
