Everyone's hyping Antigravity and Stitch right now, but Google actually launched 15 new AI tools, and some of them might be incredibly helpful for you. First, Jules, an autonomous coding agent powered by Gemini. Hook it up to GitHub and it clears your pull request backlog on its own, fixing bugs, writing tests, updating dependencies, all in the background. Second, Google Opal, a drag and drop video editor for AI, basically an AI native alternative to n8n. Describe a workflow in plain English and it turns that into a working mini app, no coding required. Third, Mixboard, it's an AI whiteboard from Google Labs. You drop different images and text onto the canvas and generate images until you get exactly what you wanted. Great for visual thinking. Fourth, Google Pomelli, it's an AI marketing tool. Just give it your website and it studies your brand, your colors, and your fonts. Then it writes and designs social media posts and ads that match your brand automatically. There are many tools on this list, just comment Google for access.
