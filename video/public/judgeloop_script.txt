There's a new prompting technique that stops Claude from telling you it's finished when it isn't.
People are using it to ship entire apps, websites and tools from a single prompt, and even the creators of Claude think this is the future of AI.
It's called the Judge Loop.
Instead of doing the normal back and forth chats, you give Claude a task and tell it to spawn a team of worker subagents.
But the secret sauce is the third line of the prompt, where you assign a judge, a prosecutor and a defense.
The prosecutor builds a case for everything wrong with your work, the defense argues back, and the judge rules on the evidence, so they loop and rebuild until the case against it falls apart.
This burns through tokens fast, so you should only build your basic prototype first and trigger the Judge Loop before you launch.
