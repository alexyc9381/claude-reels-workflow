Claude has over 90,000 skills, but you only need three of them.
First is the Anthropic Skills Library.
You just say what you're building, and it will look through its entire skills library, find the best matching ones, and even install them for you.
The second is Superpowers.
You just give it one task, and it forces Claude to slow down, properly plan, and check its own work, so you get ten X better outputs.
The third is Claude-Mem.
This gives Claude memory across every session, so he remembers your chats and files, and you never have to re-explain anything ever again.
For a list of 100 free skills, comment SKILL.
