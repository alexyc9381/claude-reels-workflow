The guy who built Claude Code just gave a talk where he gave away three things 99% of users are getting wrong and the third one will blow your mind. One. Stop prompt engineering. He says the job is now closer to being a scientist. Give the model a task that feels slightly too hard and hand it a way to check its own work. Then let it run and watch where it actually breaks. Once it breaks you fix the setup around it, not the wording of the prompt. Two. The tasks you are giving it are far too small. He handed it one simple instruction to rewrite an entire app in a different language, and it's been running on its own for two weeks straight. And three. He deleted 80% of the system prompt, not trimmed it, deleted it. Because almost every rule in there was written to babysit an older model, and Opus 5 does not need babysitting.
