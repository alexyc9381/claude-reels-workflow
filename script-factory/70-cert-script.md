resource-reveal topic the TOPIC carries the hook. Get to the value and the opinion fast; do not be clever.

## VO SCRIPT (~32s, ~150 words)

**HOOK (0-2s)**
Fourteen free Anthropic certificates. Eleven of them are a waste of your time.

**PAY IT IMMEDIATELY (2-8s)**
The three that aren't take one afternoon, and Anthropic puts their name on every one.
Not a course platform. Anthropic's own academy. One email, no card.

**REHOOK (8-16s) — the opinion**
Here's what everyone gets wrong. They do the beginner tracks, because those are the ones filling your feed.
A certificate saying you can use Claude is worth nothing the second everyone has one.

**THE TURN (16-24s)**
The three that count sit further up. Production API work. Building your own MCP servers.
That's a certificate saying you can build on Claude, not just talk to it.

**THE ASIDE (24-28s) — exclusivity, fast, then move on**
And the certification everyone keeps posting about? Locked behind a partner network your company has to pay to join.
You can't take it. This isn't that one.

**OUTLASTS (28-31s)**
The badge is the smaller half. You come out able to do the thing it claims.

**CTA (hard cut on the keyword)**
I put the three and the order in one guide. Comment CERT.
