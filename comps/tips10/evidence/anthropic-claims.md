## E1 — the account email cannot be changed
Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8452276-how-do-i-change-the-email-address-associated-with-my-account
Anthropic Help Centre, verbatim finding: it is NOT possible to change the email address associated with a
Claude account at this time. The article's own guidance is to make sure you sign up with an email you will
have long-term access to, and to contact Support from an accessible address if you lose access to the
original one.
⇒ The VO claim "the email you signed up with is permanent, Claude will not let you change it" is CLEAN.
⇒ Honesty guard: say "will not let you change it", never "you lose your account forever" — support can be
   contacted, and the article says so.

## E2 — Projects have their own memory, separate from your normal chats
Sources:
- https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11817273-use-claude-s-chat-search-and-memory-to-build-on-previous-context
- https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9519177-how-can-i-create-and-manage-projects
- https://claude.com/blog/memory
Findings: each project has its own separate memory space and project summary, kept separate from other
projects and from non-project chats. Claude's cross-chat synthesis of key insights is built from chat
history NOT including chats in projects.
⇒ The VO claims "Projects get their own memory" and "what Claude learned in your normal chats does not
   reach in there, and nothing you build inside one comes back out" are CLEAN.
