# STORYBOARD — REEL 92 JOBS (Stage 6)

> An open-source system reads every job posting for you, kills the fake ones, finds the human behind the
> portal, and writes your salary negotiation. CTA **JOBS**.
> **Format:** SINGLE DARK PANEL (house chassis, `SlopKit` Bg + Panel + ProgressBar + KaraokeCaption).
> ⛔ NEVER dual-screen.
> **Style target:** reel 58 CALLBACK. Cream frame, one dark panel, a bespoke dense EXTERIOR per beat,
> per-scene camera push, one header stating the claim, one text chip, karaoke captions under the panel.
> **VO:** `public/jobs_vo.wav` — 38.53s, 179 words, 1.0x, 11 keep-blocks spliced from an 87.7s take.

## ⛔ THE VILLAIN — APPLYVAULT
One knockoff brand for the whole reel: **APPLYVAULT**, the application portal. It is a bolted steel slot
in a door: envelopes go in, nothing comes out. It is **UNDEFEATED** from frame 0 through S6, and it loses
exactly once, at the **S7 peak**, when the Courier walks past the slot and knocks on the actual door.
No second brand name appears anywhere.

## ⛔ NUMBER SPINE (identical in every scene, sourced from the repo on 2026-08-06)
`740` postings evaluated · `62,000` stars (VO's figure; repo now reads 63.0k) · `MIT` ·
scores on the A-F / 1.0-5.0 rubric shown as **A 4.6 · C 2.9 · F 1.2** ·
ghost job = **REPOSTED 11 MONTHS · 0 HIRES** · **HEAD OF APPLIED AI** · the offer goes **UP**.
⛔ The counter in the open is **740 SENT / 0 REPLIES** and it is the same 740 as S1. One number, two uses.

## ⛔ ONE HERO ARTIFACT
**The offer number being struck out and raised.** That is what "the part that gets you paid more" promises
in the first 4 seconds, so it is the one payoff protected to the end and never spent early.
Supporting, in order: the grade plate bolting onto a door (S4), the facade falling flat (S5).
⛔ The grade plate is Claude scoring its own read, so it may never be the hero.

## ⛔ CAST
Clay Claude in four costumes, one per numbered thing, plus the reader:
**the Scout** (glasses, lantern) · **the Inspector** (stern) · **the Courier** (suit, carrying a letter) ·
**the Negotiator** (suit, at the board) · **the Reader** (prof) for the contract coda.
The man in S1 is the repo's author and is drawn as a walker, never named on screen.

# B0 — THE OPEN (0.00 → 4.51, its own build)

Frame 0 is **bright, has the subject in it, and shows a thing the viewer already dreads with zero setup**:
a colossal door with an APPLYVAULT slot, a heap of your own envelopes at its foot, and a counter reading
**740 SENT · 0 REPLIES**. Recognition, not motion.

**THREE HARD CUTS IN THE FIRST 4.5s**, camera locked in each, each advancing the problem:

| shot | at | what | why it advances |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 0.00 | Low, close on the slot. One more envelope goes in. The heap is already past the handle. | the dread, stated |
| B | 1.43 | Hard cut. The slot **CLACKS shut**, a red **NO REPLY** stamp thuds on the heap, the counter flips 740 → 741 and 0 REPLIES does not move. | the problem gets worse, and the counter proves it |
| C | 2.90 | Hard cut to the WIDE: the same door is one of hundreds receding down a night street, and far away at the end a single **gold OFFER** light is on. | the promise the VO just made, placed on screen |

⛔ Shot C is the "stay to the end, it gets you paid more" line. It **teases** the payoff by putting it at
the far end of the street. It does not show the number. The number is S8's and only S8's.
⛔ Nothing here reveals that any door is fake. That is S5's payload; spending it now is the reel-52 mistake
(the villain must lose once, at the peak).

### S0 · HOOK — `slot`, cool night · **0.00 → 4.51**
**LINE** "Stop applying to jobs until you see this. And stay to the end because the last thing it does is the part that gets you paid more."
**ONE TAKEAWAY** you are firing applications into a slot that never answers, and there is a way out at the end of the street.
**STORY** above. **WHY** the slot is the only object in the premise that every viewer has personally used.
*Rejected: a laptop with a rejection email — an interface, not a world, and it has no scale.*
**PROOF** the counter, 740 SENT · 0 REPLIES.
**GAG** the heap is already taller than the handle, so the door cannot open even if someone wanted to.
**MUTE CHECK** door + slot + heap + 0 REPLIES reads in under 2s with no audio.
**HOOK→NEXT** the gold light at the end of the street is unexplained, so you stay.

### S1 · THE AUTHOR — `court`, warm · **4.51 → 9.99**
**LINE** "A guy ran 740 job postings through this system he built, landed a Head of Applied AI role, then made the whole thing free."
**ONE TAKEAWAY** one person did the whole street by hand, and then gave away the machine.
**STORY** a walker goes down the door row with a lantern; a tally board behind him climbs to **740**; he
reaches a door that reads **HEAD OF APPLIED AI**, it opens, and then he turns and **bolts the machine to the
wall** with a **FREE / MIT** plate on it.
**ESCALATION** the tally counting is the motion; the door opening is the turn; the bolt is the payoff.
**PROOF** 740 on the tally, HEAD OF APPLIED AI on the door, MIT on the plate. All three off the repo.
**MUTE CHECK** 740 → a door opening → FREE.

### S4 · ONE, IT SCORES — `row`, cool teal · **13.34 → 17.58**
**LINE** "One. It reads every posting before you do and scores it, so you know which ones are actually worth your time."

**PROOF** the A-F / 1.0-5.0 rubric is the repo's own.
**GAG** the F door's own sign still says "ROCKSTAR NINJA".

### S5 · TWO, THE FAKES — `lot`, warm dusk · **17.58 → 21.06**
**LINE** "Two. It catches the fake listings. Some of the jobs you got rejected from were never real."
**ONE TAKEAWAY** the door you got rejected by had nothing behind it.
**STORY** the Inspector taps one door with a knuckle; it rings **hollow**; the whole front **falls flat** into
the lot and there is nothing behind it but weeds and a bare frame. Your rejection letter is still nailed to it.
**MUTE CHECK** a door falls over and there is no room behind it. That is the entire idea, in one action.

### S6 · REPOSTED FOREVER — `wall`, cool grey · **21.06 → 23.31**
**LINE** "They get reposted forever and nobody is hiring."
**ONE TAKEAWAY** it comes back, over and over, and it was never open.
**STORY** a wall of the SAME posting re-hung eleven times, each with a different date stamp, the newest still
wet. A chip reads **REPOSTED 11 MONTHS · 0 HIRES**.
**ESCALATION** the posters keep landing through the whole beat; the last one lands on the cut.

### S7 · THREE, THE HUMAN — `stoop`, warm gold · **23.31 → 29.19** · ⛔ **THE PEAK**
**LINE** "Three. And here's the part nobody expects. It finds the real person to contact instead of the application portal, and writes the email for you to send them."
**ONE TAKEAWAY** it goes around the portal and puts a letter in a person's hand.
**STORY** the Courier walks up to a door, the APPLYVAULT slot lights up expecting the envelope, and he
**walks past it and knocks**. The door opens: a hiring manager, lit warm from inside. He hands over a letter
that is already **written** — we see the subject line land, not the body.
⛔ **THIS IS THE ONLY PLACE APPLYVAULT LOSES.** The slot goes dark on the knock.
**GATE THE HOW** the letter's body is never legible. The subject line and the recipient's title are.

### S8 · FOUR, THE NEGOTIATION — `desk`, cool navy dusk · **29.19 → 34.35** · ⛔ **THE HERO**
**LINE** "And four. When the offer actually comes, it writes your negotiation, including what to say when they lowball you."
**ONE TAKEAWAY** the number on the board is not the number you take.
**STORY** an offer board under a dusk sky. The first figure is chalked up. The Negotiator strikes it through
and a **higher figure** goes up in gold. A small plate under it reads **GEOGRAPHIC DISCOUNT** with a line
through it (the repo's own "geographic discount pushback").
⛔ **THE STRIKE-THROUGH AND THE RAISE ARE THE REEL'S HERO ARTIFACT** — <2s, mute-legible, and the thing the
hook promised at 2.9s. Protect it: nothing else moves in the frame while it happens.
⛔ **NO CURRENCY FIGURE THAT IMPLIES A REAL SALARY.** The board shows the raise as a bar getting longer and a
struck-out first mark, not "$120,000 → $148,000". The repo publishes no such number and neither do we.

### S9 · THE CONTRACT — `lamp`, warm amber · **34.35 → 36.70**
**LINE** "Then it reads your contract clause by clause before you sign."
**ONE TAKEAWAY** it reads the thing you were going to sign without reading.
**STORY** a long contract scroll unrolls under one lamp; the Reader walks a **clause marker** down it and
each clause lights as it is cleared; one clause gets a flag instead of a tick.
**PROOF** the repo's `offer-prep` is a "contract reading companion — clause walk plus a lawyer question list".

## ⛔ MOTION CONTRACT (the standing failure)
Every scene gets a **continuous camera push** on top of `Panel pushIn`, because per-scene averages hide
"arrives then holds". No scene may consist of one object entering and stopping. The push must never eat the
thing the shot exists to show: in S8 the push slows to almost nothing while the figure is struck out.
