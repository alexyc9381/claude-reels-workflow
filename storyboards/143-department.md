# DEPARTMENT — full creative revision after rejection

Historical rejected staging: [September 11 board](archive/143-department-rejected-sep11.md).

Format: existing one-panel NoCodeAlex chassis, original voice and sprites.
Arc: overwhelmed solo founder -> specialized capability -> customization earns useful work.
Antagonist: doing many jobs with generic instructions; no monster or invented villain claim.
Hero artifact: a business-specific working brief that changes what the team produces.
Numbers: five departments, 45+ marketing, 17 social, UI UX Pro + Taste, eight finance, nine legal, fifty-install warning, DEPARTMENT.

## Creative boundary
The September 11 green-machine delivery is rejected. No factory bays, traveling gantry,
lever-operated press or repeated rack-fill is reused as a scene. Reuse is confined to
narration, caption timing, authentic identity, original Mascot anatomy and outer chrome.
New SVG sets and authored travel/contact/reaction drive every section.

## Hook alternatives and selection
A: OVERLOAD — one founder is already struggling with five oversized job props; the load
tips, then specialists catch and redirect each task. Clear stakes without decoding machinery.
B: PULL — five departments pull one founder between five desks. Rejected in critique:
five labeled containers and a stretched sprite would repeat the underlying defect.
C: CONDUCT — one founder conducts an ensemble of working agents. Rejected in critique:
metaphor establishment delays the benefit and risks an idle cast.
Select A for a moving probe. Authorization to iterate autonomously persists from the brief.

## Mapping
Oversized work props = the jobs a solo founder handles; tipping load = attention overload,
not a factual claim about workload; specialists catching them = skill-enabled division of work.
Storefront campaign = marketing output; shoot/edit/publish = content workflow; layout tools
changing a site = design judgment; statement/variance comparison = finance; clause inspection
and brief assembly = legal; generic outfit failing to fit = uncustomized instructions.

## Craft and sound
Four depth planes in each set: architecture, work surface, subject, cropped foreground fixture.
Key light upper left. Locked camera except the brief opening push; design reveals and team payoff use object travel.
Sprites use supported costumes and authored travel/contact/overshoot, with expression changes
only when the scene gives them a reason. Facial space is reserved; props do not cover eyes.
Sound is rewritten against these actions rather than inheriting the old factory's cue map.
Review the opening and 3–7s handoff as motion; native frames expose geometry/collision defects.
Technical scores remain separate from the creative assessment and user acceptance.

## Latest motion-direction correction

Alex rejected uniform motion speed as predictable. The action clocks in
[DeptPerformance.ts](../video/src/DeptPerformance.ts) define separate anticipation,
drive, inspection and follow-through timing for all 31 shots. Narration, captions
and cut boundaries remain unchanged. The SFX timeline uses the inverse action map.
Walking legs have actual stride phases; work leans, eye leads and recoil vary by
role and operation. Camera shutter aperture, finance/legal lens paths and the
Claude Code typing cursor are operation-specific secondary actions.
