## ⛔⛔ Rounds 1 and 2 both rejected. The diagnosis is the same for both.

| round | concepts | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | toll plaza · key vault · meter yard | "way too boring... give me pop culture ideas" |
| 2 | warp zone · the Construct · golden ticket factory, then a conveyor factory line | "no this is not good" |

Every one of those seven is a **SYSTEM**: a place with machinery in it. None has a costumed hero, a
villain with a face, or more than one location.

**What actually works on this channel** (from the reels that shipped):

| reel | world | why it worked |
|---|---|---|
| 81 DELETE | NINJA across **nine** locations | costume IS the theme, hero + blocker + turn |
| 82 BORIS | astronauts across **nine** worlds | a crew DOING something physical in each |
| 70 LANGFUSE | vault heist, agent = robber | a villain you can point at |
| 55 HAALAND | football side-scroller | a persona, instantly readable |
| 48 CHEATS | pop-culture parodies (Matrix / Bond / Saiyan) | recognition in the first frame |

The shape is **CHARACTER + GENRE WORLD + VILLAIN + many locations**. Adding detail to a system does
not fix a missing character. That is why rounds 1 and 2 failed for the same reason twice.

Note the Mascot already ships costume props — `wizard`, `cop`, `chef`, `samurai`, `suit`, `prof`,
`constr`, `glasses`, `beard` — so a costumed hero is cheap.

## My ranking

1. **THE SPEAKEASY** — the metaphor and the claim are the same sentence, and the tap wall makes 134
   a physical quantity. Best first frame of the five (a lit door in a dark alley).
2. **THE SPY** — the most locations, the briefcase is the best single "134" object, and the tux is
   the most recognisable costume.
3. **THE PRISON BREAK** — the best villain, but the palette fights the 140 luma bar.
4. **THE PIRATE** — the most fun, the loosest fit to "APIs".
5. **THE WIZARD** — literal, but closest to something already made (reel 81's ninja is the same
   "robed figure in a themed building" shape).
