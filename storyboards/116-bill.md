## §1 · THE WORLD — "THE LONG BILL"

⛔⛔ **THIS PREMISE HAS BURNED A GENRE WORLD BEFORE.** Reel 86 CANCEL lost a whole round to toll
plazas, supermarkets, subways, a night city and a **billing plant** for exactly this premise:
*"a metaphor for half the subject is not the subject."* Reel 99 then lost two more worlds with
correct mappings. The rule that landed: **use the subject's OWN objects; do not dress it.**

Point at each prop and say what it is:
- *"It's a subscription bill, and it has five charges on it."* → ships.
- *"It's a billing plant, which stands for being charged."* → cut.

So the spine object is **the bill itself** — one continuous printed invoice roll, cream paper, dark
ink rows, that crosses every scene and is physically **CUT SHORTER** five times. It is also the
keyword.

- **Spine** — the roll runs full-height or full-width through every scene, always creeping. It is
  the reel's travelling band, and it alternates **light (blank paper) and shadow (printed rows)**,
  which is the only version of a band that raises motion without lifting the black point.
- **Hero artifact** — **THE BILL**, and its receipt is the **charge counter**: `5 → 0`.
- **Villain** — the **STAMP HEAD** that keeps printing new charges. Fed in S0 and S1, still
  stamping at S18's before-state, beaten exactly once, at the peak.
- **Characters** — the clay `Mascot` in every scene, running an action loop **with a job and an
  object** (reel 110: an action loop is not a scene). Never on a mark, never a logo face.
- **Palette** — house cream `Bg` + dark `Panel`. The bill is the brightest mass in the reel; every
  room is dark around it, so the cream paper carries frame 0 without the dark stop being touched.

### Number spine (five values, all countable, none invented)
`5 CHARGES` → `4` → `3` → `2` → `1` → **`0 CHARGES`**

Each of the five tools cuts exactly one row. Rows 2 and 4 carry **no vendor mark and no figure**,
because the VO names none there — an unnamed recurring row is the honest depiction of recurrence.

| row | what it says | killed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `$20/MO · CHAT WINDOW` (no mark — VO names no vendor) | **AI STUDIO** (S5) |
| 2 | `MONTHLY` (no mark, no figure) | **NOTEBOOKLM** (S8) |
| 3 | `higgsfield` + `bytedance` · `MONTHLY` | **FLOW** (S11) |
| 4 | `MONTHLY` (no mark, no figure) | **OPAL** (S13) |
| 5 | `cursor` · `$20/MO` | **ANTIGRAVITY** (S18) |

### Hierarchy MECHANISM per section
| section | what ranks what |
|---|---|
| hook | **LENGTH** — the bill is long, and it is still growing |
| setup | **SURVIVAL** — 24 tiles, 19 struck out, 5 left standing |
| the five | **SUBTRACTION** — the counter falls, once per tool |
| peak | **COUNT** — one worker becomes a crew across three bays |
| payoff | **A STUB** — what is left of the bill after all five cuts |
