# STORYBOARD — REEL 125 AUTO (Stage 6)
> **Logline:** you have been hand-wiring automations one cable at a time, and someone already
> built 350 of them and left them on the wall, finished, free, waiting to be plugged in.
> Format:   single dark panel · chassis cloned from reel 124 MEM (`ClaudeMemReel`/`MemWorld` → `HwWorld` → `NomWorld`)
> Arc:      TRANSFORMATION with a blocker that is never beaten (`THE BENCH`)
> Villain:  **THE BENCH** — the manual patch bench where one automation takes hours. Its RULE:
>           *nothing leaves the bench finished.* Planted S0 (the hero is AT it, one cable, one
>           lamp), abandoned at S7 (he puts the pliers down — it is not defeated, it is walked
>           away from), and at S10 it **WINS**, over a whole hall of other operators still at
>           theirs, at night. It is never argued with and never destroyed.
> Hero cast: one CLAY hero (`constr` at the bench, `glasses` at the wall, `suit` at the CTA);
>           crews of 5-9 at S6/S10/S11, costumes cycled deterministically through all twelve.
> ⛔ NUMBER SPINE:  `★24,983` · `350 WORKFLOWS` · `19 CATEGORIES` · `GMAIL 26 · SLACK 9 ·
>                   WHATSAPP 8 · YOUTUBE 8` · `IMPORT` · `AUTO`
> ⛔ HERO ARTIFACT: **THE PATCH CARD** — a cream card with a chain of five wired nodes across
>                   its face, an app mark stamped at the head, five status lamps down its edge
>                   and a `.json` tab on the spine. The wall holds 350; S8 pulls ONE; S9 slots
>                   it and it runs. Everything else in the reel is machinery.

## THE WORLD — **THE EXCHANGE**

A manual telephone exchange, at night. The reason this world and not a depot, a shop or a library:
**a patch field IS a node graph.** Wiring an n8n workflow by hand is, physically, an operator
pushing a cord from one jack into another and hoping the route holds — so the metaphor is not a
costume over the subject, it is the same object drawn in brass. It also gives the reel its villain
for free (the bench where you do it by hand) and its payoff for free (a wall where it is already
done).

**The two halves of the room, and the whole argument:**

| | THE BENCH (the villain) | THE WALL (the payoff) |
|---|---|---|
| what it is | wiring one automation yourself | 350 already wired |
| light | one dying sodium lamp, cold slate around it | a lit cream field, warm brass |
| what is on it | a cord tangle, pliers, ONE unfinished card | ranked cards, every one finished |
| sound | a single ratchet, then nothing | relays, latches, a chase of lamps |

**Depth planes (every scene, via `Scene` + the `Place` table):** ceiling dark → far jack wall →
mid rack rank → the working aisle the hero stands on → **a foreground mass cropped by the panel
edge** (a stanchion, a cable trunk, the lip of a rack). ⛔ That last plane is the check for
*"is this a place or a backdrop"*.

**Light and colour, section by section** — a new light every 1.1-2.8s, neighbours differ in hue AND value:

| scenes | place | light |
|---|---|---|
| S0 | the bench, then the wall behind it lights | **cold slate + one sodium lamp** → flips to **brass + cream** |
| S1 | the slot | **brass**, hard key from the left |
| S2 | the mail bank | **teal**, cool and bright |
| S3 | the night aisle | **deep blue**, one lamp, the chair empty |
| S4 | the outfeed | **gold**, the warmest body scene |
| S5 | the marks, close | **cream-lit**, the brightest section in the reel |
| S6 | the whole field, wide | **cream + brass**, banks lighting in ranks |
| S7 | the bench again | **cold slate**, the lamp dying — a callback that changed its light |
| S8 | the card, close | **bone**, one hard key |
| S9 | the slot fires | **green**, the only green in the reel |
| S10 | the other hall | **sodium orange over near-black** — the darkest set |
| S11 | the gate | **clay + gold** |

  the Claude mark, **`★24,983`**, `350 READY-BUILT`. One object carrying the luma bar, the plate

# AS BUILT — where the shipped reel differs from this board, and why

The board is the intent manifest. Six things changed during the build; all six were driven by a
measurement or a frame strip, and they are recorded here rather than quietly edited into the cards
above, because the DIFF is the useful part for the next reel.

| the board said | what shipped | why |
|---|---|---|
| S0 is **ONE locked framing** for 5.23s | **TWO framings**, cut at f104 on the VO's own break ("so you never…") | 157f in one framing measured **5.02, STATIC** — the only failing scene in the reel. Reel 104's winning open is ONE framing for **2.57s**; this was twice that, and a long scene averages its events away. Each half now carries its own complete four-part event, so this is not §2's "four posters". |
| the bench carries **a tangle of 14 dead cords** | a **jack strip + a crossing WEB of nine plugged-in cords** | The tangle was drawn three times and read as pencils in a jar, then croquet hoops, then Olympic rings. A regular array of a simple shape reads as whatever array the viewer knows. Cords that are PLUGGED IN and CROSSING is the one unambiguous image of hand-wiring, and it became the open's biggest motion source as well as its argument. |
| the hero **strains** against the cord | he **HAULS THREE TIMES AND FAILS**, 150px each | §11: an action is a DISTANCE. The first version drove 44px on a 352px sprite — 12.5%, well under the one-third floor — and on a frame strip the three attempts were indistinguishable. Drawing the mechanism and letting it FAIL first is §12's fix, from reel 117's ingot. |
| S6 lights **19 named banks** | 19 banks light; **six names** appear, at 21px in two rows | A 19-label row rendered as an unreadable smear over the top rank. §4 underneath it: the depiction is the banks lighting and the total climbing, and **text is not a depiction**. |
| S10's operators are `Crew` on WORK loops | **`Hero`** with heat, stern, strain and the hero's own failed-haul cycle | `Crew` forwards neither `heat` nor `stern`, so the angriest room in the reel had a cast with no faces. ⛔ And giving them a standing `strain` without an authored action DROPPED the scene 7.71 → 6.66, because `Hero` scales its loop by `1 - max(drive,strain)*1.7`: the strain silently cut the loop and put nothing in its place. |
| S2's bank is **556px on the wall** | **690px at eye level**, holes lifted | On a contact sheet the object the sentence is about was the smaller thing in frame, and the whole-reel darkening pass had taken the holes brown-on-brown. An empty bay is a bright cream plate. |

**Unchanged and worth saying so:** the world, the villain and its rule, the hero artifact, the
number spine, the honesty ledger, the scene count, every scene onset, and the decision to show no
fifth mark on the word "Stripe". The board's two hardest calls — the world and the two unbacked
claims — both survived contact with the build.

# ROUND 1 — THE MARKS-FORWARD REBUILD

Alex, after the first delivery: *"don't have any text [in the hook], just have
all the different logos... really focus on the logos instead of trying to use
unique animations... same with the categories, have different icons... same with
the n8n workflow part, really focus on showing a graphic of an ACTUAL n8n
workflow."*

**This is a change of AXIS, not of quality**, and it supersedes the scene cards
above wherever they conflict. THE EXCHANGE survives as the room, the villain and
the arc are untouched, and the honesty ledger is unchanged — but the objects the
camera stops on are now REAL APP MARKS and a REAL n8n CANVAS instead of drawn
stand-ins. `feedback_real_marks_are_the_props`: at half a second on a phone a
viewer recognises a mark; they do not decode a silhouette.

- **S0** is a 24-socket board filling with real marks, no text in the panel; shot
  B is six marks wiring themselves together.
- **S1** adds a shelf of seven apps. **S2** is Gmail in, four real apps out.
  **S4**'s outlets ARE their marks. **S5** ejects four mark tiles with their real
  counts. **S6** is 19 icons for 19 categories, no labels.
- **S8/S9** are an actual n8n canvas — dot grid, rounded-left trigger, real app
  icons in the nodes, port dots, the trailing `+`, and an execution pulse.
- ⛔ `R.roster` is 24 apps, each GREPPED against the repo tree, ordered by how
  often it really appears. Stripe is in the VO and is still not on screen.
- ⛔ **S7 keeps its cable and has no finished marks in it at all.** That is
  deliberate and it is the reel's one contrast: the hand-built scene is the only
  one with nothing recognisable in it.
