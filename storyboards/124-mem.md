## THE HONESTY LEDGER (verified live 2026-08-27, before a frame was drawn)

Source: Anthropic, *"Claude's memory works everywhere, and you decide what's in it"*, **2026-08-25**.

| on screen | what it actually is | sourced? |
|---|---|---|
| cream folders in labelled bays | memories are stored as **a list of files under Topics** in Memory settings | ✅ verbatim |
| the press stamping while he talks | Claude **"adds topics to memory as you chat, instead of summarizing conversations after they end"** | ✅ verbatim — this is the actual change |
| the file pulled open and rewritten | users can **"read, edit, or delete each one"** | ✅ |
| three feeds: browser · desktop · phone | on **web, desktop and mobile** | ✅ |
| the `FREE · PRO · MAX` stamp | on by default on **Free, Pro and Max** | ✅ |
| the cut line at the local shed | Cowork tasks run **in the cloud** with access to chat memory; a task that needs **local files** runs on your machine instead | ✅ |

⛔ **THE VO SAYS "FOR ALL USERS" AND THE FRAME MUST NOT.** Team/Enterprise memory is
admin-controlled and defaults **off** for individual users. The plate therefore reads
`FREE · PRO · MAX` — the three plans it is actually on. This is the standing rule that when a VO
asserts a result you cannot source, you dramatise the MECHANISM and stop at the edge of the claim.

⛔ **"DELETE OBSIDIAN" IS THE VO'S HYPERBOLE, NOT A CLAIM THE PICTURE MAKES.** The crate carries the
real Obsidian mark because it is the noun in the spoken line and a viewer recognises a mark in half a
second — but nothing on screen says the product is worse, broken or gone. The crate is a vault kept
**by hand**, and what replaces it is a thing that fills itself. That is the whole comparison.

## THE WORLD — **THE MEMORY WORKS**

A working records plant. Not a library: nothing here is browsed, everything here is **made**, and it
is made *while you are still talking*. Cream files against dark bays, brass machinery, teal light.

**Depth planes (every scene, `WorldKit.Surface`):** ceiling haze → far bay wall → mid rack rank →
the working line the hero stands on → **a foreground mass cropped by the panel edge** (a stanchion, a
hopper lip, a rail). ⛔ The last one is the check for "is this a place or a backdrop".

**Light and colour, section by section** — a new light every 2-4s, neighbours differ in hue AND value:

| scenes | place | light |
|---|---|---|
| S0 | the empty floor | **violet-cold**, one hard key → flips to **teal + brass** when the wall lights |
| S1-S2 | the intake line | **teal**, low raking |
| S3-S4 | the old bench, over THE GAP | **cold slate**, one dying lamp, the gap unlit |
| S5 | the press | **brass + hot sodium**, the only warm scene before the wall |
| S6-S8 | the topic wall | **cream-lit, the brightest section in the reel** — this is the payoff |
| S9-S10 | the three outlets | **sky blue** |
| S11 | the works at full gear | **gold** |
| S12 | the barrier | **red**, everything else killed |
| S13-S14 | the cloud run vs the local shed | **sky above / dead slate below** |
| S15 | the gate | **clay + gold** |

### SCENE 0 — 0.00 to 3.93s (3.93s) · LOCKED WIDE, ONE FRAMING · **HOOK**

- **SET:** the empty floor of the works, violet-cold. One hero dead centre on a lit disc, nothing else
  standing on the floor — `reel-hook-simplicity`: **a hook is an IMAGE, not a room.** The bay wall
  behind is dark and unreadable. A stanchion crops the left frame edge.
- **CAMERA:** locked. Slow in-panel push 1.00 → 1.055 only.
- **BLOCKING (the four-part event):**
  1. **BEFORE (f0, settled):** the hero holds a **crate of hand-kept notes overhead**, the Obsidian
     mark on its face, paper spilling over the lip. He is visibly failing: `strain 0.85`, the crate's
     lid **bows** (weight is DEFORMATION, not size), plates of paper wobble, and **steam lifts off his
     head** — the emitter goes on the stillest part of a pressing sprite.
  2. **TRIGGER (f22, on "Delete"):** his arms give.
  3. **TRAVEL (f22-38):** the crate falls **the full panel height** — 560px, 3.7× its own height, well
     past §11's one-third floor.
  4. **ARRIVAL (f38):** SLAM. It bursts: 40 loose sheets thrown on ballistic arcs, a dust ring, the
     floor recoils, the hero staggers back and lands on his heels.
  5. **THE PAYOFF (f44-104), overlapping the burst, never queued:** the dark wall behind him **lights**,

     `shock` → `cheer`, and looks up at a thing that built itself.
- **CLAIM PLATE:** `CLAUDE BUILT ITSELF / A SECOND BRAIN`, cream, ≥18% of the panel, settled at f0.
  ⛔ It sits on the **back wall**, not on the crate — a gate carried by the wrong object deforms that
  object, and the crate has to stay a crate.
- **SFX:** the heaviest stack in the reel. strain groan → `rebuild_thud` + `sub` on the slam →
  paper scatter ×3 → an **ascending run** of `mech_clank` as the drawers punch in.
- **TAKEAWAY:** you were carrying it. It now carries itself.

### SCENE 2 — 6.45 to 8.00s (1.53s) · WIDE, LOW · SETUP

- **BLOCKING:** files slide off the end of the bench one after another and drop into THE GAP. They do

### SCENE 4 — 10.76 to 12.13s (1.37s) · TIGHT ON THE LEVER · ESCALATE
- **VO:** *"...every time you switch chats."*
- **§3 test — the verb is SWITCH:** a throw-over lever. Not a fade, not a cut: a **mechanism**.
- **BLOCKING:** the hero throws the lever, the loaded chat pane is **yanked sideways out of frame**
  and a blank one slams into its place — and everything standing on the old pane is tipped into THE
  GAP on the way past. **It happens TWICE**, the second 40% faster and louder. He flinches on the
  second; `heat` 0.35 → 0.75, ticks pop, steam.
- **TAKEAWAY:** the villain wins, twice, unopposed. Peak of the problem.

### SCENE 5 — 12.13 to 14.36s (2.23s) · MEDIUM TWO-PLANE · **THE TURN**
- **VO:** *"Now Claude saves details dynamically in the background."*
- ⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS THE ACTUAL MECHANISM AND THE WHOLE SCENE IS ITS SIMULTANEITY.** Anthropic's own
  wording is "adds topics to memory **as you chat**, instead of summarizing conversations after they
  end", so the shot has to be two things happening AT ONCE in one frame.
  - **FOREGROUND, left:** the hero mid-sentence, `act=1 WORK`, a speech ribbon paying continuously
    out of him toward the right. He never looks behind him.
  - **BACKGROUND, right:** the ribbon feeds a **press**. Every 14 frames the ram comes down, stamps a
    file, and a chute posts it into the wall. Four files in the scene, on the beat, while he talks.
  - ⭐ A SCENE IS ONLY AS ALIVE AS ITS DEADEST HALF — the talking half gets the ribbon (continuous
    travel) so neither half is ever static.
- **AND IN THE SAME BEAT:** steel plates swing across THE GAP and bolt down. The villain is floored,
  not killed. `heat` back to 0.
- **TAKEAWAY:** it is being written *now*, not later. That is the news.

## THE EIGHT DEFECTS THE STILLS FOUND, AND WHAT EACH ONE ACTUALLY WAS

Every one was found by rendering and LOOKING, not by a gate. Six of the eight
were sitting behind green numbers at the time.

1. **The claim plate was painted over the subject.** A full-width cream card at
   panel y 96..272 sat on top of the crate the entire hook was about, and the
   hero's two forearms — correctly ending ON it — read as two clay sticks
   hanging in mid-air. ⭐ Fixed by reel 110's rule: a gate carried by the wrong
   object deforms that object. The luma and plate jobs moved onto `WorksSign`,
   an enamel board bolted to the wall at a different x.
2. **The hero was 20% of the panel and the prop was smaller**, over two thirds
   of dead floor. Hero to 344px, and the load became a TOWER taller than he is.
3. **The set was a backdrop.** Measured: luma 153 (fine) and **10.1% saturated
   pixels**, with a wall of pale grey boxes. The `floor` palette went bone and
   brass and the back wall got a real `RackSkeleton` standing EMPTY at frame 0 —
   because "it built itself" needs something to have been built. **10.1% → 50.6%.**
4. **The tower's unit index was inverted.** `start = GY - (N - i) * UNIT` put
   unit 0 at the top and unit 4 on the floor, so the Obsidian mark meant for the
   crown rendered at ankle height and the stack collapsed UPWARDS. Algebra could
   not see it; one still could.
5. **Three fixes to the villain's lip were written into the wrong file.** `Gap`
   lives in `MemWorld`, the patches went into `MemProps`, and a string-replace
   that matches nothing reports success. ⭐ The fix that finally worked was not a
   value fix at all: a hole in a flat-lit diorama does not read from VALUE, it
   reads from a HAZARD-MARKED EDGE.

## ROUND 7 — THE HEADER IS ON FRAME 0

*"make sure the hook header is there since 0:00 of the video? not just coming in
at around like 0.2 seconds ish"*

⛔⛔⛔ **This was house chrome, not this reel.** `SlopKit.SectionHeader` opened
with `settle = over(f, 0, fr(0.34), Easing.out(Easing.back(1.3)))` driving
opacity, a -16px translate and a 0.9→1.0 scale. At `f = 0` that is opacity **0**.
The same component draws every section band, so it drew the first one too —
**every reel in the house opened on a frame with no header and popped one in at
~0.2s.** 117 files import `HookHeader`.

Fixed with an `at0` prop that pins `settle` to 1, passed on the FIRST band only
(`at0={cur === BANDS[0]}`); mid-reel band changes keep their pop, because they
are not frame 0 and nothing is lost by animating them.

pill is the largest white object in the frame, and every HOOK_LUMA reading ever
