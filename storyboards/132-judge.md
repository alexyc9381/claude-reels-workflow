# STORYBOARD — REEL 132 JUDGE (Stage 6)

> **Logline:** Claude signs off its own work, so the work is hollow — the judge loop puts that
> work on trial with a prosecutor, a defense and a judge, and loops it until a strike leaves
> no mark.
> **Format:** single dark panel · clone `ClaudeLibrary130Reel.tsx` (reel 130) chrome verbatim
> **Arc:** TRIAL (villain → vindication). The villain wins the hook and is not beaten until S13.
> **Villain:** **THE HOLLOW EXHIBIT** — work that *looks* finished. Its RULE: it is never
> destroyed, only *caught*. It is still on the evidence shelf, still hollow, in the last frame.
> **Hero cast:** THE WITNESS (clay, no costume) · THE JUDGE (`prof` + `stern`) ·
> THE PROSECUTOR (`suit`, tint dark) · THE DEFENSE (`suit`, tint light) · THE FIRM
> (8 sub-agents, `suit`/`glasses`/`beard`/`girl`/`cop`/`chef`/`fro`/`prof` cycled by
> `costumeFor(i)`) · THE CLERK (`girl` + `glasses`).
> ⛔ **NUMBER SPINE:** the 3-line docket (line 3 is the one that matters) · 3 nameplates
> `JUDGE · PROSECUTOR · DEFENSE` · a 10-segment accuracy rail (**no numeral**) · 3 loop passes ·
> keyword `JUDGE`.
> ⛔ **HERO ARTIFACT:** **THE EXHIBIT** — a brass-framed slab of work on a plinth. Hollow at the
> hook, refilled at S1 *with one bay left dark*, flagged at S10, defended at S11, ruled at S12,
> and at S13 the gavel comes down on it and leaves **no mark**. Everything else is decoration.

## THE HOOK — three concepts, built, scored, one picked

Per [`docs/THE-OPEN.md`](../docs/THE-OPEN.md) step 1, three genuinely different 76-frame shots are
built and measured with `tools/hook_score.py` before the body is authored. All three clear the
three bars from [[feedback_the_verb_must_have_logic]]:

| | name it in 2 words | can a BODY do that to it | ⭐ IS IT THE SUBJECT |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · THE OATH** | "witness box" | he stands in it, grips the rail, swears | Claude testifying that its own work is good |
| **B · THE STACK** | "claim stack" | he stacks it, it bows the rail | each claim Claude makes about the work |
| **C · THE SEAL** | "the seal" | he drives it down with both arms | Claude certifying its own work "done" |

### A · THE OATH *(the design; the picked cut is recorded in the log after scoring)*
One Claude in the witness box, dead centre, right hand raised. Beside him on the plinth, at chest
height, **THE EXHIBIT**: a brass-framed slab with a cream face and a green PASSING lamp. It reads
finished. **A causal chain of five events, each caused by the last, starting with a BODY**
([[ANIMATION-QUALITY §31.4]]), and **every beat sits on a word at `onset − 4`**
([[feedback_the_picture_leads_the_voice]]):

```
f0   (settled)   hand at the top of the oath arc · exhibit WHOLE · green lamp ON
f6   "new"       the left hand comes DOWN on the oath block          ← the BODY starts it
f11  "prompting" the strike rings the plinth; panel 1 pops loose and FALLS
f26  "stops"     panels 2 and 3 blow out; the green lamp DIES
f32  "Claude"    the exhibit, lighter, TIPS on the plinth — his hand is still up
f44  "lying"     the whole remaining face falls away · HOLLOW: two bare struts, nothing behind
f66  "face"      he turns his head and looks at it for the first time — hand STILL RAISED
```

⭐ **Anticipation, to the definition in [[feedback_a_wobble_is_not_a_clock]]:** a predictable
threat (the face is coming off), a visible countdown (panel 1, then 2 and 3, then the rest), and
a denied payoff until the word "lying". ⭐ **One cause, four visible effects** (§31.3): the oath
strike sheds a panel AND kills the lamp AND tips the plinth AND scatters the exhibit tags.
⭐ **Discrete strokes, not ramps** (§31.2) — every one of those is a `BACK` stroke with overshoot.

**Silhouette:** the exhibit is bright cream on near-black oak; the witness is saturated clay on
the same dark ground. Both subjects are the LIGHT side. **Proportion:** exhibit ≈ 58% of panel
width (air on both sides, under the 85% line); witness ≈ 340px against a 792px panel.
⛔ **The frame-0 gates ride on the plaster band and the clerestory shaft, NOT on the exhibit**
([[feedback_relight_depends_on_the_mover]] · THE-OPEN's "a gate carried by the wrong object
deforms that object"), so the exhibit is free to be the size it should be.

### Header (constant across all cuts)
```
CLAUDE SIGNS OFF        ← line 1, ink
ITS OWN WORK            ← line 2, clay
chip:  THE JUDGE LOOP · 1 MIN SETUP
```
It states a **claim**, not a description, and the chip carries what you GET
([[feedback_the_band_states_the_value]]). 4 words a line.

   light direction, listed per card. Nothing floats on black.

   house in-panel push, and `cam.rot` is dead in this chassis
