Built on the **CALLS engine** (one illustrated WORLD authored once + CAMERA-MOVED per scene). Cinematic heist-noir: warm-vs-teal grade, volumetric light, haze, film-depth, dense sets, dimensional clay characters, camera-as-character. **⛔ NO emojis. ⛔ NO neon (the red ALARM is deliberate). ⛔ NO dark "tech UI" panels / raw screenshots.** Every "tech" idea is a physical prop. ## THE STORY (one sentence)
Your Claude Code agent is a **masked robber** looting your vault in the dark and you can't see a thing — until you install **Langfuse**, the **surveillance system** that films every move, replays the whole heist frame-by-frame, and catches the exact move that stole half your gold.

## THE WORLD (authored once, camera pans L→R, x ≈ 0..2900)
1. **THE VAULT CHAMBER** (x 0–1000) — moody bank vault: circular black vault door cracked open on a shining **gem** + **stacked gold bars** (your tokens), safe-deposit walls, columns, pipes, marble floor, drain grate. The heist set. (S0–S3)
2. **THE CAMERA RIG** (x ~950) — where **Langfuse** is installed: a wooden crate stencilled `langfuse` opens into a rig of **CCTV cameras + a monitor** that bolt onto the vault. (S2)
3. **THE MONITOR WALL / EVIDENCE ROOM** (x 1000–2050) — a control desk facing a wall of **security monitors** + one big screen: the recorded heist laid out as a **timeline / strip of frames**. The owner-mascot (you) sits at the desk in a headset. (S4–S9)
4. **THE LANGFUSE HQ LOBBY** (x 2050–2900) — a warm lobby: the carved **repo monument** (`langfuse/langfuse ★31.5k`, brass star-coin counter), wooden badges **FREE · OPEN SOURCE · MIT**, and a **crowd of 30,000 owner-mascots**. (S10–S11)

## CAST
- **THE ROBBER = your agent** (clay mascot, beanie + balaclava w/ eye-slit + striped jersey + gloves, swag sack). Always masked; you never see his eyes clearly = the black box.
- **THE OWNER = you** (clay mascot, no mask, a detective's flat cap + headset from S5). Installs the cameras, watches the tape, catches him.
- **LANGFUSE = the surveillance system** (CCTV cameras, the monitor wall, the tape). The hero tool.
- **THE VILLAIN = the blind spot** (the dark, sealed vault) — beaten the instant the cameras turn it to glass (S2/S5).

## NUMBER SPINE (locked, real, identical everywhere)
**100** moves · tokens = **gold** · one screen = the **security tape / timeline** · **0.49s** + **$0.000037** on one move's receipt · one move stole **half (50%)** the gold · rewind to the exact broken frame · **★31.5k** · **30,000** developers · comment **LANGFUSE**.

- **Mute-read:** coins vanishing into a drain, then the vault seals to black.
