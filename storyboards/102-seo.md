## 4. The three floors

1. **Every scene is a real place.** Named location (THE NIGHT AUDIT), 5 depth planes, one light
   direction per scene, world props throughout. ✅
2. **The camera is disciplined.** Every scene is LOCKED; the only movement is the mandated continuous
   in-panel push, which is a scale on the whole panel, not a camera move. Zero pans, zero handheld.
   One subject moves at a time in every scene. ✅
3. **The arc has a shape and the payoff is not spent early.** ✅ — curve below.

**Intensity curve** (peak must beat the hook, no belly sag):

```
S0 hook  9.0  ####################
S1       6.0  #############
S2       6.5  ##############
S3       8.5  ###################        <- the hero artifact, the turn
S4       8.0  ##################
```

low point is S7, which is the villain's own scene and is immediately answered by the peak 0.7s later.

## 5. The adversarial critic pass (mandatory)

Ran against the board above. Five things it broke, all fixed in place:

| # | the critic's objection | verdict | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **S1 and S2 are the same rack twice** — the CALLBACK S1=S2 failure. | UPHELD | S2 rebuilt on a different set with different objects: the AI answer card and the map card. They now share no base object. |
| 2 | **The payoff is spent at S3** — the ladder is the hero artifact and it lands at 7.8s. | PARTLY | S3 delivers the ORDER; S8 delivers the RESULT (every page lit, every flag green). Different payoffs, and the intensity numbers hold (8.5 then 10.0). The ladder returns at S8 *complete*, so S3 reads as a promise and S8 as its settlement. |
| 3 | **The villain loses more than once** — the swarm at S5 already beats "one page at a time" before S7 introduces it. | UPHELD, structurally | The villain is declared LATE by the VO's own order, so it cannot be undefeated-until-peak. Board now declares the arc as DISCOVERY with a single-scene villain, rather than claiming a villain arc it does not have. S5 shows a roster, not a race; the head-to-head happens only at S7→S8. |
| 4 | **Swipe check, 0-5s** — is there a repeat in the first five seconds? | UPHELD | Four hard cuts at 0.00 / 0.93 / 1.87 / 2.93s, then S1 at 3.87s: five distinct framings, none re-stating the one before (card → storm → console → page → whole rack). |
| 5 | **S8 is 21 frames — can a payoff land in 0.70s?** | UPHELD as a risk | It can only land if it is ONE event with no sub-beats. The board therefore forbids a push on S8 and gives it a single three-frame strike. If the render shows it reading as a flash rather than a reveal, the fix is to move the S8 boundary 6 frames earlier, not to add beats. |

Also checked: **mirror violation** — n/a, single panel, not split-screen. **Repeated base object** — the

## 6. Build notes carried forward from reels 99-101

- ⭐ **Ceiling'd component idles on every prop** — a drifting `Sheen` on each page, a ≤2px content
  drift, a real pendulum SWAY on the hanging flags pivoted at the punched eye (≤1.7°, ~2.3s period).
  All ≤3px or ≤0.06 opacity. This alone took reel 100's median motion 8.26 → 9.80.
- ⛔ **Write the width arithmetic before the box.** Mono at weight 800 advances ~0.60em. The longest
  agent filename is `seo-dataforseo.md` (17 chars) — at 22px that is 225px of text, so its plate is
  260px inner, and every plate carries `textOverflow: ellipsis` + `minWidth: 0`.
- ⛔ **No coloured glow, no low-opacity washes.** Both greps must return 0 before any render.
- ⛔ **The Mascot's body rect IS its face** — no mark on his chest. Marks go on the rack banner, the
  card faces and the floor.
- ⛔ Every SFX `at` is ROOT seconds and every one is a measured onset from `vo/seo/words_final.json`.
