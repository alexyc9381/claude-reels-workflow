# STORYBOARD — REEL 106 SKILL (Stage 6)

> **Logline:** four pasted prompts turn an ordinary Claude Project into a tutor that drills you,
> dates you and grades you — so the thing you have been "learning for months" finally finishes.
> **Format:**   single dark panel · clone the **ClaudeFreeReel (105)** chassis verbatim — cream
> `StudioBg`, dark `Panel`, `KaraokeCaption`, `HookHeader`, progress rail, clay `Mascot` from `SlopKit`.
> **Arc:**      TRANSFORMATION / build-a-system (value-first). See STORY-ARCS.md.
> **Villain:**  **NONE — deliberately.** `feedback_outlier_lift_is_within_creator_only` measured
> external villains at rel-median 1.00, never above 1.38x, while build-a-system with an INTERNAL
> enemy is the only breakout family (7.19x). This VO hands us the internal enemy in plain words:
> *"not because they're slow, but because nobody's teaching them correctly."* The enemy is an
> **EMPTY CHAIR**. It is never personified, never fought, and it is beaten by being SAT IN.
> **Hero cast:** **STUDENT Claude** (`glasses`) — the viewer's stand-in, on screen from f0.
> **TUTOR Claude** (`beard`) — enters at the TURN (f291) and never leaves.
> ⛔ **NUMBER SPINE:** `6 MONTHS` → `4 HOURS` (hook) · cards **1 · 2 · 3 · 4** on the board ·
> **7** day-blocks · **45 MIN** · `SKILL` (keyword, hard cut on it).
> ⛔ **HERO ARTIFACT:** **THE PINBOARD.** Four empty card slots, legible from the first body frame,
> one filled per step, carrying the 7-day roadmap at the peak. It is what makes 1-2-3-4 read as
> **stages of one job instead of a list** (`free105-reel` round 7: a list has no visible GOAL, no
> ACCUMULATION and no PAYOFF; the download bar is what fixed it there, the pinboard is it here).

## 0. THE FACT POSITION — read before drawing a single frame

The VO is a repackaging of a viral X/Medium post ("Claude has a hidden mode called Infinite
Personal Tutor… any skill from zero in 4 hours"). Three of its four claims are drawable as-is.
One is not, and one names a real feature we must NOT depict.

| the VO says | reality, checked 2026-08-14 | what the picture may do |
|---|---|---|
| "open a new **project** and paste this prompt … in the **instructions**" | ✅ **REAL.** Claude **Projects** are self-contained workspaces with a **project instructions** field that "further tailors Claude's responses"; free accounts get 5. | Draw it. This is the one place literal product language belongs — **ONE** text chip: `PROJECT INSTRUCTIONS`. |
| "this one hidden mode in Claude **called a personal tutor**" | ⛔ **NOT A FEATURE.** Claude ships nothing named "Personal Tutor" or "Infinite Personal Tutor". The name comes from the viral post. | The tutor is a **CHARACTER who sits down**. ⛔ NEVER a Claude UI element, toggle, menu row or badge reading "Personal Tutor". The "mode" is the state the Project is in *because of what was pasted*. |
| — (adjacent, unspoken) | ⚠️ Anthropic **does** ship a real **"Learning" style** (Socratic; style dropdown; to all users 2025‑08‑14). | ⛔ **DO NOT DRAW THE STYLE DROPDOWN.** It is a *different mechanism* from the one this VO explains. Depicting it would illustrate the wrong product and make the reel wrong in a way a knowledgeable viewer spots instantly. |
| "master any skill in **4 hours** which would usually take **6 months**" | ⚠️ **UNSOURCED.** A claim from the post, not a measured result. | It may sit on the **claim plate** — stating the reel's claim is the hook's job. ⛔ **No evidence furniture:** no verified tick, no testimonial, no source badge, no counter or receipt that "proves" it. Dramatise the MECHANISM, stop at the edge of the claim. |
| "Claude will build a precise **7 day roadmap** … **45 minutes** per day" | ✅ This is the **output the pasted prompt asks for**. | Draw it as Claude's output. It is the hero artifact. |
| "Claude will **evaluate** your learnings" | ✅ Evaluation is just what the 4th prompt asks for. | A **seal / tick**, never a fabricated score. ⛔ No "98%", no grade letter, no pass rate. |

⛔ **And the reel never shows the prompts themselves.** `gate-the-how`: the prompt cards are
face-DOWN or edge-on, legible as *objects*, never as readable text. The keyword is the gate.

## 1. THE WORLD — "THE NIGHT CLASS"

One long study table under a single hanging lamp, in a dim night-school room after hours. Two
chairs. The student's side is buried; the other side is **empty**.

**Why this world and not a more exciting genre.** `ANIMATION-QUALITY.md` §5 and
`feedback_real_marks_are_the_props`: when a world is boring the answer is *the subject's own
objects, BIGGER* — reel 104 lost a whole build reaching for a pit lane, a hangar and a rocket.
Every object below is named by the VO itself.

| on screen | what it actually is |
|---|---|
| the tower of textbooks | "6 months" of the way you learn now |
| the single thin card beside it | "4 hours" |
| the **empty chair** opposite the student | "nobody's teaching them correctly" |
| the tutor who sits down in it | the Project once the first prompt is in its instructions |
| the four slots on the pinboard | the four prompts, and how far through you are |
| the grey textbook shoved off the table | "explaining concepts like a boring textbook" |
| the lit situation box the student is thrown into | "real situations" |
| red mistake marks thinning to none | "until making mistakes becomes impossible" |
| the date clamp biting the table | "you must have a deadline" |
| 7 blocks across the board, each with a short bar | "7 day roadmap … 45 minutes … per day" |
| the seal pressed on the returned card | "Claude will evaluate your learnings" |

**Depth planes (≥4, every scene):** (1) table edge + foreground props · (2) the two chairs and
who is in them · (3) the pinboard wall · (4) the window with night outside + a row of dark empty
desks · (5) the lamp cone itself.
**Light:** ONE committed direction — a hard warm cone straight down from the hanging lamp onto
the table; cool night blue from the window as the counter. ⛔ Light is a shaped cone, never a
full-frame fill (`THE-OPEN` rejected draft 3).
**Palette:** house matte — CLAY `#D2724E`, GOLD `#E7B24C`, GREEN `#3F9E74`, INK `#1A1813`, cream
`#ECE9E2`. ⛔ No neon, no glow, no `boxShadow: 0 0 Npx` (the grep gate is 0).

## 4. THE ADVERSARIAL CRITIC PASS (mandatory — run, and recorded)

**Swipe points, 0–5s.** 0.0 tower + card present, ratio legible with no narration · 1.0 card floods
to clay · 2.6 hard cut to the oppressive low angle · 3.4 card rises and holds · 4.5 the tower
collapses. ⛔ First draft had the collapse at f141 (the last word) with nothing between f109 and
f135 — **a 0.9s hold in the most-watched second.** Fixed: the collapse now STARTS at f135 with the
tower's spine pre-buckling from f119, so the second half of the hook is continuously in motion.

**Repeated base-object.** ⚠️ **CAUGHT ONE.** S3, S5, S7 and S9 are all "a card travels and slots
into the board" — four scenes on the same base action, which is the CALLBACK S1=S2 failure four
times over. **Fixed by making the slot the SECOND event in each, never the first:** S3's event is
the folder unfolding, S5's is the textbook being shoved off, S7's is the clamp biting, S9's is the
seal. The card slot is a 10-frame beat inside each, not the scene.

**Payoff spent early.** ✅ The board is not full until f1023. The roadmap does not exist before f862.
⚠️ S0 shows the *promise* (tower → card) but not the *method*, which is the correct split.

**Villain integrity.** N/A by design — no villain (§ header). The empty chair is the enemy, it is
"defeated" exactly once, at f322, and the defeat is a character sitting down, not a fight.

**Intensity curve.** ✅ Plotted above; no sag, peak beats hook.

**Mirror violation.** N/A — single panel, not split-screen.

**⚠️ The critic's remaining objection, recorded and accepted:** S3 (4.60s) and S9 (4.57s) are the
two longest scenes and both are "a card arrives". They survive because their real events (the
