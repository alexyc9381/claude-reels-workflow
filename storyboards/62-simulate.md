## HERO CHARACTER — **CLAUDE THE ARCHITECT** (the god)
A colossal, mythological deity version of the Claude mascot, **seen from behind**: broad glowing shoulders, a flowing
cosmic robe, a faint halo of orbiting stars that subtly forms the Claude asterisk, warm **clay-gold rim-light**
(`#D2724E`) against a deep indigo cosmos. He hovers over / cradles a glowing **ORB-WORLD** (the simulation). Zeus /
architect-of-worlds energy. Recurring observer + hero across all 6 scenes.

**The ORB-WORLD:** a living snow-globe civilization of tiny Claude sprites — a town where a *life plays out* (houses,
market, farms, roads, a calendar tower). The world = **an IDEA playing out** (council-lane, not life-upheaval): the icons
are a **lightbulb / rocket-launch / blueprint / strategy-chart** (an idea / a plan / a strategy) — broad but idea-focused.

**Recurring cast** ([[reel-knockoff-references]]): **THE UNPLANNED-FOR** — a parody-safe kaiju / black-swan monster
= "the thing you didn't plan for" (recurs as the disaster in every failing timeline). **Townsfolk sprites** — a builder,
a merchant, a little-family sprite (react: cheer → panic → flee with tiny drawn suitcases). **Living world** — a comet,
crops, weather, a market, small fires.

## SCENE CARDS

### S1 · HOOK — THE WATCHER (9s)
1. **LINE/DUR:** hook line · 9s.
2. **TAKEAWAY:** Claude is a god that runs your decision as a whole *living world* you can watch.
3. **STORY + WHY:** frame-0 = the Architect (settled, complete) from behind, cradling the glowing orb; **3 beats** —
   (A 0–3.5s) slow push toward his shoulder, halo-stars drift; (B 3.5–6.5s) we cross INTO the orb: the town, sprites
   walking, calendar tower reads **MONTH 1**; (C 6.5–9s) one sprite stops at a crossroads and looks up — a drawn "?"
   glows (the open loop). **WHY:** pure pattern-interrupt, single cinematic hero, mute-legible (giant deity + tiny
   world = "a simulation"). *Rejected:* opening on a Claude UI "running a sim" → vibecoded, no awe, fails the hero rule.
4. **PROOF:** the calendar tower "MONTH 1"; the god's face IS the Claude glyph. HeroHeader over it.
5. **ESCALATION:** opener — but never static: 3 evolving beats (god → into-orb → the choice).
6. **GAG:** a tiny townsprite waves UP at the giant god; the halo-stars snap into a faint Claude asterisk.
7. **TRANSITION/SFX:** cold open · deep cosmic sub-boom + a shimmering world-hum · rising whoosh on the push-in.
8. **MUTE CHECK:** giant Claude god + tiny living world + "MONTH 1" → "he's simulating a life." ✓

### S2 · CONJURE — ONE PROMPT → A WORLD (7s)
1. **LINE/DUR:** reveal line · 7s.
2. **TAKEAWAY:** you give ONE decision (a blurred, locked prompt) and the god conjures the whole situation around it.
3. **STORY + WHY:** the god's hand descends; a **BLURRED prompt-tablet** floats up — "should I ______?" blurred + a
   DRAWN padlock (the gated lead-magnet). He flicks it into the orb → the world BUILDS ITSELF around the crossroads
   sprite: houses, roads, a market, **family-sprites**, **coin-stacks (the money)** snap into place; universal decision
   idea icons flash — **lightbulb / rocket-launch / blueprint / strategy-chart**. **WHY:** the tiny-input→whole-world contrast is
   the wow; gates the how (prompt blurred, world sharp); the icons broaden TAM to *any* life decision. *Rejected:*
   typing into a chat box → vibecoded; the god conjuring a world is the hero action.
4. **PROOF:** the blurred locked prompt-tablet + the coin-stacks/people forming ("the situation"). Calendar MONTH 1.
5. **ESCALATION:** one sprite (S1) → a whole populated world snapping into being. Busier, bigger.
6. **GAG:** a sprite pops into existence mid-yawn, confused, dusts itself off; the moving-truck honks.
7. **TRANSITION/SFX:** match-cut on the god's flick · a "materialize" sparkle-swell + little pops as buildings snap in.
8. **MUTE CHECK:** blurred locked prompt → a whole world builds → "one prompt makes the whole simulation." ✓

### S3 · THE BREAK — IT CRASHES (8s) — *drama peak-build*
1. **LINE/DUR:** break line · 8s.
2. **TAKEAWAY:** the world plays forward and CRASHES — and you see the EXACT moment.
3. **STORY + WHY:** the calendar tower spins **MONTH 1 → 2 → 3** (number spine climbing); the town thrives (crops grow,
   coins stack, sprites happy) → **THE UNPLANNED-FOR** rises on the horizon and strikes: one building topples → a

   retention; the "exact week" is the concrete receipt paying off the hook. *Rejected:* a line-chart dropping → cerebral;
   a living town wrecked is mute-legible + felt.
4. **PROOF:** calendar MONTH 3 + red **WEEK 14** collapse stamp + the domino chain.
5. **ESCALATION:** thriving world (S2) → cascading catastrophe; biggest motion so far; a new threat (the kaiju).
6. **GAG:** the kaiju roars then trips over a tiny house; one sprite calmly sips coffee as it all burns (this-is-fine).
7. **TRANSITION/SFX:** calendar-spin whoosh → ominous rumble → the WEEK-14 stamp = a hard bass IMPACT + glass-crack.
8. **MUTE CHECK:** thriving town → monster → collapse + "WEEK 14" → "the plan breaks at week 14." ✓

### S4 · THE RERUN — EVERY WAY IT FAILS (6s) — *chaos peak of the reel*
1. **LINE/DUR:** rerun line · 6s.
2. **TAKEAWAY:** it's not one guess — the god RE-RUNS it dozens of ways, each failing differently.
3. **STORY + WHY:** the god snaps → the shattered orb REWINDS (time-rewind streaks) and replays, breaking a DIFFERENT
   way → then the frame MULTIPLIES into a constellation of orbs, **RUN 1, RUN 2 … RUN 40** counter climbing, each a
   mini-world failing its own way (fire / flood / the family leaving / bankruptcy). Wall of failed timelines. **WHY:**
   the escalation + numeric peak; shows the SYSTEM (every way) = the innovation-bar payoff. *Rejected:* a single replay

4. **PROOF:** the **RUN 40** counter + the grid of failing orbs.
5. **ESCALATION:** one failing world → FORTY. Visual + numeric apex.
6. **GAG:** the god face-palms as orb #7 also explodes; a drawn "L" tombstone pops on each dead world; one sprite gives
   a thumbs-up right before its orb blows.
7. **TRANSITION/SFX:** finger-snap → rewind-zip → rapid multiply "pop-pop-pop"; a rising arcade fail-stinger per pop.
8. **MUTE CHECK:** many worlds each blowing up + "RUN 40" → "it tried 40 ways, all broke." ✓

### S5 · THE VERDICT — THE ONE FIX (6s) — *the turn / catharsis*
1. **LINE/DUR:** verdict line · 6s.
2. **TAKEAWAY:** the god finds the EXACT crack and the ONE change that makes the world survive.
3. **STORY + WHY:** the grid collapses back to ONE orb; the god leans in, a glowing red **FAULT-LINE** traces to the
   single first domino (the root cause) under a spotlight; he reaches in and SWAPS it (pulls the red domino, sets a
   green one). Time replays — the kaiju strikes but the world HOLDS: the chain stops, the town stabilizes and glows
   **gold**; a **SURVIVED** seal stamps. **WHY:** resolves the arc; the payoff is *physical* (a spotlit domino, a swap),
   not a cerebral "root cause: X." *Rejected:* a text bullet "root cause" → cerebral; the domino swap is mute-legible.
4. **PROOF:** red fault-line → the ONE swapped domino → **SURVIVED** seal; calendar runs past WEEK 14 safely.
5. **ESCALATION:** chaos (S4) → a single precise fix that HOLDS.
6. **GAG:** the god dusts his hands; the kaiju sits down and pouts; a sprite plants a drawn "we made it" flag.
7. **TRANSITION/SFX:** grid implode-whoosh → a precise "ting" on the swap → warm major-chord swell + seal thunk.
8. **MUTE CHECK:** red crack → swap one piece → world turns gold "SURVIVED" → "find the one fix that saves it." ✓

5. **ESCALATION:** triumph/resolution; the [[reel-progress-bar-reward]] seal lands here.

## ⛔⭐ CINEMATIC DIRECTION — this reel is a SIGNIFICANT leg-up over prior reels (Alex 2026-07-17)
> *"the animations, backgrounds, shots and camera angles genuinely have to be breathtaking and cinematic — even more of a significant improvement over my previous videos."*
NOT the flat "panel-sticker" look. Every scene is a CINEMATIC SHOT with real camera language, volumetric light,
and deep parallax. The single dark panel is a **cinematic window / letterbox** into these worlds, not a UI card.

**CAMERA (a distinct move every scene — the panel content transforms in 3D-feeling space):**
- S1: slow **dolly push-in** past the Architect's shoulder → accelerate INTO the orb (scale race from god to tiny world).
- S2: **crane-down + orbit** as the world builds; rack-focus from the blurred prompt (foreground) to the world (back).
- S3: **whip-in / handheld shove** as the disaster hits; a hard **push** onto the WEEK-14 crack; subtle screen-shake on impact.
- S4: **snap pull-back** to reveal the 40-orb constellation (tiny → vast); slow lateral drift across the failing worlds.
- S5: **macro push** onto the single red fault-line domino (extreme close), then ease out as the world heals + glows gold.
- S6: **hero crane-up** as the gold orb rises toward camera; settle to the CTA. Each cut = a real camera transition, not a fade.

**LIGHT (cinematic, volumetric — the "premium" split, never flat single-tone):**
- Deep-space key: warm **clay-gold god-light** ([[reel-hook-header]] `#D2724E`) from the Architect vs cool indigo rim on the world.
- Volumetric **god-rays** from behind the Architect; the orb has real **inner glow + caustic light** on its glass; bloom on hot points.
- Atmospheric haze / depth-fog so distant sprites read softer (atmospheric perspective); soft contact shadows under every hero.
- Disaster (S3-4): firelight + red emergency wash; Verdict (S5): a single spotlight beam on the domino; CTA (S6): golden hour glow.

**DEPTH (multi-plane parallax — 4-6 layers per scene, each moving at its own rate):**
- Foreground silhouette layer (orb glass rim / Architect's shoulder / dust motes) → mid hero → world → far nebula/starfield.
- Parallax float per layer (`Math.sin(lf/…)` amplitudes scale with depth); DOF blur on fore + far planes; drifting particles/embers.
- Backgrounds are RICH: layered cosmos gradient + nebula wisps + starfield + slow drift — never a flat fill.

**SCALE:** lean into the epic god-vs-tiny-world contrast — the Architect is colossal and mostly off-frame; the worlds are jewel-tiny.
Wireframe = throwaway scaffolding; ship bar = "would this stop me dead on the feed and look like a film." Overhaul until yes.
