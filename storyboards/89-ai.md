| | |

| 0.93-3.43 | the fall, bursting through every past conversation on the way down |

| 3.43+ | he bounces back out, spraying coins, and it keeps pouring until he is under it |

**Header states the VALUE, not the problem:** `GIVE CLAUDE / INFINITE MEMORY`.

**The physics is solved, not keyed.** The board is a cantilever: fixed at the root,
free at the tip, continuous release, then a damped cosine as it rings down. Him and
every coin run through ONE ballistic solver with restitution, so bounce heights
decay by a coefficient rather than by taste. The landing frame is `invFy()` of the
drop distance — solved, not guessed.

### Four defects only measurement caught

- **he was floating 50px above the plank.** The Mascot's legs end at y=184 of a
  200-unit viewBox, so its feet are at **0.92** of the box, not 1.0 — and `shock`
  values near 0.35 add a built-in 34px hop (`jump` in SlopKit). Standing him at
  `deck - size` with `shock=0.3` levitated him. Feet go on the deck; standing shock
  is 0.
- **the ballistic solver dropped him through the floor.** Starting exactly ON the
  floor moving down solves time-to-contact as 0, and the free-fall guard then
  returned a below-floor position. Contact has to be resolved before that guard.
- **the camera cancelled his bounce.** A camera rigidly locked to the subject makes
  the subject motionless by definition. The bounce was solving correctly and
  measuring **zero** on screen. The camera now lets go at the landing and settles on
  the pit; the bounce measures 135px.
- **`LEVELS.*` are linear gains, not dB.** `LEVELS.SFX_TEXTURE - 3` is `0.112 - 3`,
  a negative volume — Remotion throws only at the frame the cue starts, so
  `remotion still` passed and `remotion render` died mid-way. Offsets must be
  `* db(-n)`.
