## §1 · THE WORLD — "THE THREE TABS"

**⛔⛔ THIS PREMISE GETS NO GENRE WORLD.** Reel 86 burned three hook sets and fifteen scenes on toll
plazas, supermarkets and billing plants for exactly this premise; reel 104 lost two rounds to pit
walls and hangars. `feedback_real_marks_are_the_props`: **use the subject's own objects, bigger.**

The subject's own objects here are **the three websites themselves**. So the set is one oversized
**browser** living on the house Panel, and the props are **real captures pulled live from the three
sites** (`public/shots/`, captured this build). Alex asked for real screen recordings; the honest
world and the requested world are the same world.

- **Set** — one dark browser window, full-panel, with a **tab strip that advances between beats**
  (the reel's spine: three tabs, three platforms, left to right).
- **Hero artifact** — the **Arena leaderboard row**, real, with Claude at rank 1. The reel's receipt.
- **Characters** — a clay `Mascot` Claude at the keyboard, reacting per landing (`THE-OPEN` law 2:
  characters stop scrolls). Never on a mark, never a logo face.
- **Background process, every shot** — the real leaderboard scrolling, a render bar sweeping, the
  model band travelling. No shot is one hero doing one gesture.
- **Palette** — house cream `Bg` + dark `Panel`. Real captures keep their own colour; they are the
  only saturated things on screen, which is what makes them read as REAL.

### Hierarchy MECHANISM per beat
| beat | what ranks what |
|---|---|
| 1 | a **leaderboard** — 391 models ordered by score, the literal hierarchy |
| 2 | **repetition** — the same charge landing again and again is the cost, not a number |
| 3 | **containment** — many engines seated inside one box |
| 4 | **resolution** — low-res → photoreal is a quality ladder |
| 5 | **level** — two bars that end equal |

### Number spine (all real, all captured)
