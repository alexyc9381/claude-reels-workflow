## The three floors

1. **Real place, not shapes on black.** Every scene sits on the same film stage: sprocket rails,
   perforated foot, live scrubber and timecode. Ground colour shifts per act (slate for proof,
   clay for the steps, green for the payoff) so the reel travels without changing world.
2. **Disciplined camera.** Locked in the hook (five hard cuts). Body scenes use the house `Panel
   pushIn` only — one continuous push, no per-scene invention.
3. **The arc has shape.** Intensity `9 · 6 · 7 · 5 · 4 · 5 · 6 · 6 · 8 · 7 · 9 · 8`. The peak
   (SKILLS) beats the hook, the belly never drops below 4, and the hero artefact is not handed
   over until GENERATE at 25s.

## Critic pass

- **Swipe points 0–5s:** five hard cuts, a new fact on each. No repeat framing.
- **Repeated base object:** the logo grid appears in HOOK-B and STEP 3 — deliberate callback, and
  the second one is a *selector* not a wall. No other object repeats.
- **Payoff spent early?** No. The hook shows `FREE`, but the ARTEFACT (the running app, the
  terminal) is withheld until 25s.
- **Number spine:** 400+ / 14 / 25,757 / 4,527 / MIT / 3 / 200+, identical everywhere.
- **Belly sag:** HOWTO at 0.86s is the lowest point by design — it is a breath, and it is the
  shortest scene in the reel.
