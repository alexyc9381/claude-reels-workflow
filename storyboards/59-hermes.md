### S0 HOOK — goldfish faints to zero
- **Takeaway:** Your AI has amnesia: the second you close the chat it forgets everything you taught it and reboots to zero, so it can never actually learn you.
- **STORY (top):** Claude mascot as a GOLDFISH in a bowl. A clay cursor clicks the chat's red [X]; the fish's memory thought-bubble (icons of your project/style/rules) drains empty, eyes swirl, it faints and respawns blinking "new here!" Pure symbols: fish + bowl + X + emptying bubble = it just forgot you. The famous "3-second goldfish memory" trope does all the explaining for free.
  - **WHY (this, not that):** Goldfish is the single most universal shorthand for "no memory" on Earth, and it's a CHARACTER (obeys the mascot-must-act-it-out rule) that can faint and react for the laugh, unlike a prop. REJECTED: an Etch-A-Sketch shaken clean — reads as "erased" but it's a cold mechanical OBJECT, no cute stakes, no face to root for, breaks the costumed-hero rule. REJECTED: a whiteboard being wiped — generic office cliche, zero charm, not <2s craveable. REJECTED: a Men-in-Black neuralyzer flash — organic/complex prop, the flash hides the subject and needs a beat to parse. Goldfish wins: living, funny, instant, and the 3-sec-memory meme is pre-installed in every viewer's head.
- **PROOF (bottom):** Bottom third = a knockoff "Claube" chat UI mid-conversation ("remember, my project is..."). The cursor hits [X]; the window reopens snapped to "New chat" with a red badge reading "0 messages remembered · Memory: 0 KB." Named receipt on screen = "0 messages remembered" plus the bowl's pet-store tag "AttentionSpan: 3 sec." Two hard numbers (0 and 3s) = credibility that it truly resets to nothing.
- **Escalation:** It's the cold-open, so it escalates from silence/black straight to peak relatability at frame 0: no logo, no warmup, just the exact pain (my AI forgot everything again) slammed into view with a fainting fish. Sets the floor at ZERO memory so every later scene can climb — the next reveal only lands because we established the fish starts with nothing.
- **Gag / cameo:** Cameo: a Dory-blue tang parody drifts past in the background going "...have we met?" (Finding Nemo memory-loss nod). Knockoff receipt: pet-store price tag "GOLDFISH $0.99 · AttentionSpan 3 sec · memory not included," and the chat brand is knockoff "Claube." Living-world micro-gag: on the [X] click the fish's eyes spiral, it belly-ups and faints for a half-beat, then blinks back to life reading a name-tag that says "Hi, I'm NEW."
- **Transition + SFX:** Cut IN hard from black on a wet "BLOOP" as the fishbowl drops into the SpotCone; the [X] click lands a sharp "CLACK," then a descending "boo-woop" drain-gurgle as the memory bubble empties and the fish faints. Open loop hook: the emptied bubble hangs there flashing "???" — a tiny gold glimmer flickers at the bowl's rim (first hint of Hermes) as we smash-cut to S1 on the question "...but what if it didn't forget?"
- **Mute check:** Stranger, sound off, <2s: sees a Claude-faced goldfish in a bowl, a cursor smacking the chat's red X, its memory bubble draining blank while it faints, and "0 messages remembered." Reads instantly as: AI forgets everything the moment you close it. No sentence needed.

It shuffles to the door and — BONK — stomps the exact same garden RAKE it stomped yesterday, handle cracking its face, stars spinning. ### S3 HOW — the save-point (save+reload)
- **Takeaway:** Here's the actual mechanism: after every job Hermes quietly files what worked and what flopped, then loads all of it back before the next job — so your AI keeps its progress instead of respawning brain-dead every run.
- **STORY (top):** Claude-the-quest-knight clears a dungeon (job done), stands on a glowing SAVE PAD, and a floppy-disk beam sucks up his two trophies — a green ✓ "worked" gem and a red ✗ "didn't" gem — SAVED. Then a fresh Claude respawns and the beam pours both gems back into him — LOADED — before he charges the next door. Symbols only: door → save pad → floppy eats ✓/✗ → poured into new hero → loop arrow. It ACTS out "keeps what it learned every run" the way every gamer already reads a save point.
  - **WHY (this, not that):** The VO names TWO motions — "saves what worked/didn't" AND "loads it all back before the next" — so the hero object must perform BOTH in one glance. A save point does exactly that: you save AT it and you respawn FROM it. It's also geometric/iconic (obeys the geometric-refs-not-organic-blobs rule) and the floppy is the single most mute-obvious "your progress is kept" symbol on Earth. REJECTED: a diary/journal the mascot writes in — it collides with the memory.md PROOF (same object rendered twice, killing the story-symbol ≠ real-UI split), and journaling is a slow low-energy motion with no snap. REJECTED: a USB stick plugged in/out — too literal, reads as "backup," not "learns from wins-and-mistakes," zero character stakes. REJECTED: a photocopier — shows duplication, not the ✓/✗ judgment, and has no reload-before-next beat. Save-point wins because gaming's "you keep what you learned each run so you don't repeat the death" IS the Hermes loop — it even lets the red ✗ gem BE yesterday's bug from S2, now filed so it can't recur.
- **PROOF (bottom):** hermes-memory.md open in the editor, two green "+" gutter lines appended live under a fresh timestamp: `## job #0472 — auth-refactor ✅` / `+ worked: reused lib/session.ts helper → one-shot` / `+ avoid: DON'T regen the schema (broke 3 tests yesterday)`. Status bar receipt: `memory.md · 47 → 48 entries · +1 saved (0.3s)`, with a right-side toast `↺ 48 memories loaded before next job`. Named on-screen numbers: 47 → 48 entries, +1 saved (0.3s), 48 loaded — and "broke 3 tests yesterday" is the literal receipt for S2's repeated mistake.
- **Escalation:** S2 was uncontrolled LOSS — token pile on fire, groundhog-repeat of "the same mistake you fixed yesterday." S3 flips that exact pain into controlled GAIN: yesterday's bug is now the red ✗ gem, captured and FILED so it physically cannot repeat, and the frantic re-explaining becomes a crisp 0.3s auto-save. Motion escalates from passive forgetting to an active two-way beam loop stamped with receipts. Ends OPEN: as the reload pours smarts back in, the corner $ counter starts dropping — teasing S4's "because it remembers, it stops wasting money / run heavy thinking on a cheaper model."
- **Gag / cameo:** Cameo: the SAVE flash throws up a knockoff 8-bit title card "FINAL FORGETSY VII" (Final Fantasy parody) with a spinning crystal — a jab at the goldfish memory. Micro-gag: the red ✗ "didn't work" gem is shaped like a tiny fainting goldfish (callback to S0/S1) that tries to flop away off the pad — the save-beam yoinks it right back in. Payoff read: "even the Ls get saved."
- **Transition + SFX:** Cut IN on a hard "dungeon door WHUMP + QUEST-CLEAR jingle," slamming out of S2's chaos. SAVE = the iconic floppy "ka-CHUNK" plus a coin-collect blip. RELOAD = an 8-bit power-up rising arpeggio + sparkle shimmer. OUT = a soft pre-echo "cha-ching" as the $ counter ticks down, pulling the ear into the money scene.
- **Mute check:** Stranger, sound off, <2s: sees a classic glowing green SAVE POINT + floppy disk eat a ✓ gem and a ✗ gem (SAVED), then pour them back into a fresh hero (LOADED), with a loop arrow — instantly reads "the AI keeps what worked and what failed and reloads it every run." The bottom memory.md "+1 · 48 entries" confirms it's real AI memory, not just a game gag.
- **Base object:** THE SAVE POINT — a golden retro-RPG save pedestal with a giant chunky floppy disk hovering over it, ringed by a green "SAVE POINT" glow and a two-way light beam. (Distinct from S0/S1 goldfish bowl, S2 burning token-pile, S4 cost dashboard, and the later Obsidian vault / cron clock. The one object that natively performs BOTH verbs the VO names — save AND reload.)

### S4 LEARNS YOUR STYLE — the bespoke tailor
- **Takeaway:** Hermes doesn't just store facts ABOUT you, it learns HOW you like things and produces work already shaped to your taste (no em-dashes, your stack, your voice), so you stop re-explaining your preferences every single job.

Symbols only, no sentences up top: a tailor + a made-to-measure suit hugging your shape = "it makes work that fits YOU" = it learned your style.

REJECTED #1: a MIRROR or PARROT that mimics you. Tailor wins because mirror/parrot are passive copy-cats (and organic blobs that break the geometric-reference rule), reading as "repeats you back," not "applies your taste to NEW work"; a tailor actively CRAFTING a fresh garment in your fit shows the agent producing new output in your style, and a parrot would repeat the S0 goldfish's animal register. REJECTED #2: a fingerprint/signature stamp. Tailor wins because a fingerprint reads as identity/who-you-ARE (that's a fact), not how-you-like-things-DONE, and it's static, dropping energy; the tailor's busy hands escalate motion and set up the couture-label pun on the reel's own HERMES name.
- **PROOF (bottom):** BOTTOM screen = a knockoff CLAUDE.md / Claude settings pane titled "style.md - learned from you," lines auto-checking as it watches: "no em-dashes ......... ✓", "plain words, no jargon ✓", "TypeScript > JavaScript ✓", "short commit messages .. ✓", "tone: dry, direct ..... ✓".
