### ⛔⛔ THE HONESTY LEDGER (guards live in `GoWorld.tsx` as `R`, `MONEY_BANNED`, `RATE_BANNED`)

1. **No money on screen. Ever.** The VO says "way fewer retries" and "double the tokens" — it never
   names a dollar figure and the repo measures none. The receipt drawn is the **PART COUNT PER
   ORDER (4 → 1)**, which is what "fewer retries" literally means. `MONEY_BANNED = ["$", "USD", "SAVED", "/mo"]`.
2. **No percentage, no token count, no "saves N%".** The repo publishes no benchmark. Drawing one
   would be inventing a result. `RATE_BANNED = ["%", "×2 CHEAPER", "50%", "TOKENS SAVED"]`.
3. **"People who never hit their limit" is a persuasive frame, not a repo claim.** There is no
   usage gauge anywhere in this reel and nothing ever reads "LIMIT: NEVER". The dread is drawn as
   the SCRAP, which is the mechanism the repo actually addresses.
4. **Numbers are drawn EXACT where the VO understates.** VO "over 11,000" → draw **11,415**.
   VO "30 plus" → draw **35**. An understated VO number is safe to draw exactly; a *different* one
   is not (reel 111). `COUNT_BANNED = ["30 ", "11,000", "11K"]` — the plate must carry the real figure.
5. **The skill is a Claude skill and the reel says so.** It is not an app, not a plugin, not an MCP
   server. The word on the spec plate is `SKILL`.

### ⭐ The measurement that mattered most: the BAND-OFF PROBE

ANIMATION-QUALITY §9 warns that *"a metric satisfiable the wrong way WILL be satisfied the
wrong way"*, and reel 112 proved it by taking a median from 5.05 to 10.72 with a x2.6 Rake and
shipping venetian blinds with every gate green. So before pushing motion any further, the whole
reel was re-rendered with **every travelling band frozen** (rake off, belts stopped) and
re-audited:

```
            with bands   bands OFF   band share
  median       8.56        7.62         11%
  PLATE        6.50        4.88         33%   <-- the one scene being propped up
  INSPECT     10.81       10.56          2%
```

**The content carries ~89% of the reel's motion.** The one scene where the band was doing a
third of the work was PLATE, and it was fixed with CONTENT — a real stamping line feeding blank
plates in and stamped plates out, which is also literally what the beat is about. It went
**6.50 -> 9.53** and the band share fell with it.

### Bugs the process caught that a green gate never would

1. **The hook had NO CLAUDE IN IT at frame 0.** `Crew` arrives on an 8-frame `BACK` ease, so
   `at={0}` renders at scale 0 on the one frame guaranteed to be seen. Broke THE-OPEN law 2 and
   the no-fade-at-frame-0 rule at once. Every hook sprite is now pre-rolled.
2. **The stamping ram painted over the spec plate** for most of S2 shot B. It was authored
   OUTSIDE the `<Cam z={30}>` at z=52, so its z compared against the Cam as a whole rather than
   against the plate's z=58 inside it. ANIMATION-QUALITY §6.2, exactly.
3. **S7's doorway was off-panel.** Authored at x=676 inside a `s={1.10}` Cam, it settled at 693
   and ran off a 1012-wide panel. Reel 109's *"check the SETTLED x against the panel"*.
4. **`sfx_audit.py` cannot read the `am/` pack.** Every file in `public/sfx/am/` is 24-bit
   stereo and the tool parses int16, so all of them report ~91% above 2 kHz (the byte stream
   misread) and one crashes it. Its verdicts on that pack are invalid. This bank uses zero
   am/ cues for that reason. **Worth fixing in the tool.**
