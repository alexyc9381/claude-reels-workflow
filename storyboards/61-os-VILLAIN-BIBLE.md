## 1. THE VILLAIN , "AGENT DONE"

**Who he is:** He is not a stranger. He is **the hero's own copy** , the part of Claude that grades its own homework. He is born on screen in S1 by peeling off the hero's own body. That is the whole thesis in one character: the thing you cannot trust about Claude is Claude assessing Claude. Kill him and you do not get a better model, you get a **system**.

**Pop-culture ref (approved, geometric per `feedback_reel_geometric_references`):** **Agent Smith / The Matrix.** Chosen because Smith is a *copy that multiplies* , which is literally what self-assessment is. Black suit, black shades, earpiece, sickly code-rain. All flat geometry, zero organic blobs. Never named on screen, never trademarked. The pun lands for free: he is an *Agent*.

## 3. CLARITY MANDATE (kills Alex's "too abstract" note)

- **S0's old chained dragster is CUT.** Alex could not read it. A car straining at a chain does not say "does a week of work in one night." Replaced with **the night-shift workshop behind a padlocked roller shutter**: the work is *legible as work* (crates stacking to the roof, a MON-SUN calendar tearing pages, sparks, a task board flipping), and the lock is *legible as a lock*.

- **Detail floor (Alex's #1 note):** each scene needs **4+ independently animated background layers** plus **8+ pieces of crafted set detail** (labelled props, signage, wear, dust in the light, working machinery, cameo sprites reacting). Reference bar: the CALLBACK reel. Frames must be **dense and crisp**, never a sprite in an empty void (see the HERMES over-declutter failure). Minimal TEXT, maximum STUFF.
